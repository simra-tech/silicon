# Custom CML divide-by-2 no-bleed transient series V1

This package preserves three physical-model transient experiments on the exact
no-bleed Candidate V3 netlist. It includes every executed deck with its native
log and binary raw plot. The candidate is identical in all three runs:

`d5900dae655f376e6c2aeeebe18d7753f3c36c73283ca859586e57f25dcedd52`

## Experiment boundary

All runs use the nominal 27 °C IHP SG13G2 model sections, physical
self-heating, `VCC_HBT=2.5 V`, `VSS=0 V`, complementary 0 V to 1.2 V clocks
with a 200 ps period, and a 2 ps transient output interval. There is no reset,
initial condition, or node-set.

The series contains:

| Directory | Stop time | Intentional testbench difference |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `unloaded-2ns-v1/` | 2 ns | no external output load |
| `unloaded-4ns-v2/` | 4 ns | horizon extension of the first deck |
| `loaded-4ns-v1/` | 4 ns | adds matched `1 mA` ideal DC sinks from `DIV2_P` and `DIV2_N` to `VSS` |

The 4 ns unloaded deck differs executably from the 2 ns deck only in the stop
time and raw-output name. The first 1,143 rows before 2 ns are numerically
identical across their binary plots. The loaded deck differs from the
unloaded 4 ns deck only by the two matched sinks and raw-output name.

## Independently checked observations

`RAW-SUMMARY.tsv` was generated by parsing the binary plots directly and
checking every scalar for finiteness.

- The 2 ns unloaded run contains 361 variables by 1,144 points. After 200 ps,
  the master collector, master follower, and slave collector differentials
  change sign 11, 5, and 7 times respectively. The external output has no
  crossing and stays at or below zero. This short startup window is
  inconclusive.
- The 4 ns unloaded run contains 361 variables by 2,284 points. From 2 ns,
  the master collector and master follower differentials stay positive. The
  slave collector differential makes 20 crossings with an all-two-crossing
  full-cycle mean of `199.97724266752945 ps`; the output makes no crossing and
  remains between `-0.18407894043558648 V` and
  `-0.044507643871527947 V`.
- The loaded 4 ns run contains 363 variables by 2,284 points. From 2 ns, the
  master collector differential makes 20 crossings with an all-two-crossing
  full-cycle mean of `200.0018311605495 ps`. The slave collector and output
  make no crossings. The output remains between
  `+0.30252501420703637 V` and `+0.69131935778643905 V`.

The approximately 200 ps internal cycles match the input-clock period, not an
observed divide-by-two terminal waveform. Matched output sinks did not produce
a divided output; they moved input-rate differential motion from the slave
collector to the master collector and selected the opposite one-sided output
polarity. That localizes a load-sensitive state-transfer question but does not
establish its cause.

Each native log retains the temperature-limiter NaN and heat-sink warning.
OpenADA 0.4.0 legacy control mode completed each requested evidence envelope,
but its `pass` field is structural execution evidence only. The preflight
records say `assertion_evaluated: false`. Project engineering status remains
unknown.

## Publication corrections

Two generated reports and a follower TSV are intentionally excluded. The TSV
has 16 headers but 17 fields per row, and one prose extremum conflicts with
the corresponding binary point. A later output-node report used false
physical line numbers and treated selected transient device-current vectors
as a demonstrated node-KCL closure. None of those artifacts is required for
the raw-backed observations above.

`SOURCE-IDENTITIES.tsv` binds the frozen private artifacts to the public
copies. Candidate, native logs, and raw plots are verbatim. Decks replace PDK
paths with `$PDK_ROOT`. OpenADA JSON, preflight records, and generated control
scripts replace runtime workspace, tool, catalog, and temporary paths with
portable relative paths or named placeholders.

## Reproduction

Set `PDK_ROOT` to the IHP SG13G2 PDK root. From any run directory:

```sh
ngspice -i -n -o reproduced.log *.public.cir
```

Move the retained raw plot first if it must not be overwritten. Compare a
reproduction by circuit identity, plot metadata, vector names, numeric
payload, and warning inventory; raw timestamps may differ.

No function or performance pass, specification closure, signoff, or tape-out
readiness is claimed.
