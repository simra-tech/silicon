# G1 revision r4 adopted as the chip of record (2026-09-28)

**Decision (principal engineer, 2026-09-28):** `blocks/g1_padring/layout/g1_chip_top_1414_r4.gds`
(SHA-256 `225d0b535321ed312594bb13d97b7271014f487162564a56c874572de715173a`, 84 156 088 bytes, top
`g1_chip_top`, committed in `9b4e1b6ea`) replaces r3 (`7d07a784…`, committed in `30f5192a`) as the chip of
record. Canonical CDL: `blocks/g1_padring/netlist/g1_chip_top_1414_r4.cdl`
(`e060c0c59a168e5b69b0c47dc31cbfe4f4444d2ec8af62960c339c1230a2c99f`). r3 becomes the retained fallback, as
r2 was for r3. Every number below is **simulated** or a physical-verification result; nothing has been
fabricated or measured.

## Why: red-team finding F1

[`redteam-20260927/trip_path/FINDINGS.md`](redteam-20260927/trip_path/FINDINGS.md) F1 (MAJOR): on r3 the
hard comparator trips 46–48 LSB early (tt, block bench) and 53–59 LSB early (ff/−40 °C, r3 chip deck). The
cause is a 0.20–0.34 ns race: the inverter `XCLKI` puts the hard strobe just after the soft comparator
starts its reset. The error is systematic but depends on temperature. So r3 needed per-part, per-temperature
calibration (H6 with a bench k(T) table), and in the joint mismatch screen the room-calibrated hard
threshold drifted by more than 0.5 mV at 125 °C in 4 seeds.

## What changed (r3 → r4)

- **TRIP macro.** In the cell `__rz_port_text_033_retained_g1_trip`, `XCLKI` is replaced by the 20-device
  non-overlap generator `g1_novclk`. Each comparator evaluates on an undelayed `cmp_clk` edge and resets
  only after the other has decided. Pins, outline (229 × 207 µm), placement and sub-cells are unchanged.
  Design: [`blocks/g1_trip/layout/candidates/nf4_novclk/README.md`](../blocks/g1_trip/layout/candidates/nf4_novclk/README.md).
- **GatPoly fill.** 58 rectangles (5/22, 406.0 µm²) in the top cell, outside every macro box. GFil.g
  margin 918.98 µm² (r3: 618.07 µm²).
- **XOR evidence.** XOR r3 → r4 with the TRIP box (771, 736)–(1000, 943) µm: outside the box only 5/22
  changed (+58 polygons, 406.0 µm², 0 text differences); before the fill nothing changed outside the box.
  The TRIP cell in r4 equals the candidate macro (XOR 0 on 48 layers, texts equal). The digital macro is
  byte-identical to r3 (cut cells `183a3496…` both; XOR 0 on 31 layers)
  ([signoff-1414r4-20260927](../blocks/g1_padring/reports/signoff-1414r4-20260927/README.md)).

  with the dev PDK; hard threshold finer than ±3 % at ss/ff on the chip deck; calibration rehearsal at

- **Open:** a bond map bound to the r4 hash in `padframe/` is not written; the tracked
  `bondmap_20260926_r4.csv` is bound to r3, and a hash-only copy bound to r4 passed `verify_bondmap.py`.

## Consequence for calibration (spec §6)

The hard threshold now sits at its code: per-part room calibration remains required (sense-amplifier
offset, mismatch spread about ±2 mV), H6 remains as restated, but k(T) becomes a per-part fine correction,
not the r3 compensation of an 8–12 mV kick offset. Expected bench calibration codes at 25 mV: hard about
126–134, soft about 128–130 (r3: 170–172 / 128–130).
