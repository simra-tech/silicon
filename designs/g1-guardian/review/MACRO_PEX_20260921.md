## Leakage numerical-floor audit

Stricter reltol1e−6/abstol1e−18/vntol1e−9/gmin1e−18 in36pad-inclusive DOSE sweeps (`macro_20260921T155935Z_2a61abed`) causes many DC operating-point failures at the−0.3V starting bias. A separate0→10mV sweep (`macro_20260921T160119Z_90b4a508`) completes28/36 points; eight remain failed. Across those28 completed zero-gate points, the largest current change versus baseline is1.314fA. The largest relative change is39.5% for cold/slow/high-rail HV device current, changing0.301fA to0.182fA. Thus extremely small intrinsic leakage values are numerical-floor-sensitive; the much larger pad leakage still dominates this fixture. The stricter screen is incomplete and does not qualify allPVT leakage. Raw failures and the comparison table remain in their campaigns.
