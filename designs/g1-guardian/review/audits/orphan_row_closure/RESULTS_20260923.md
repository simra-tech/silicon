`sg13g2_tiehi` instance. Revision 2 reconnects all 53 VDD terminals by adding

## Preserved failure and revision

Revision 1 added a redundant two-cut stack through Via1/2/3/4 and TopVia1.
Its connectivity and ten individual cut-open tests passed, but stock main
DRC failed with 26 additional markers: eight each V2.b/V3.b/V4.b and two
TV1.a. Existing native VDD upper cuts were already present; new upper cuts
were too close or merged into an incorrect cut shape. Its GDS, proof,
stock report, and marker delta remain preserved. Revision 1 must not be used.

Revision 2 starts again from the exact original parent, adds only the two
Via1 cuts, and leaves all existing upper metal/vias intact. Original same-run
graph ownership witnesses are rebound to their exact native M1/M2 polygons;
cross-run numeric cluster IDs are not treated as equivalent identities.

The independent saved-GDS audit selects the historical 53 terminals, then
freshly probes all 54 windows / 54 actual native polygons in the current
full flat conductor graph. Every polygon reaches the same VDD root; VSS
remains separate. Each of the two new cuts is then individually removed from
an exact 8 × 6 µm native ROI, and a surviving M1-to-TM1 path is verified.
This local positive-path witness complements the separate global root proof.
An initial report/inventory assertion incorrectly carried revision 1's
57 polygon probes into revision 2; the independent recount failed that
assertion and the report was corrected. Both saved audits always retained
their actual per-polygon records; no geometry or passing audit was changed.
