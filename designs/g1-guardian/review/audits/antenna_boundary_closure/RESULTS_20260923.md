# Early VDD antenna connection

One added Metal3 rectangle removes all nine antenna markers in the matched,
sealed R5 assembly: the unchanged stock antenna deck reports **zero markers**
in 104.559 s. This is not final R6 or full-chip signoff. Original failures are
retained. No device, source, model card, rule deck, port or native cell changed.

## Exact binding and mechanism

The three marked sites are `pad12_en`, `pad14_sclk` and `pad15_sdi`, each
`sg13g2_IOPadIn.XI0` / `sg13g2_LevelDown.MP0`. The pinned CDL record is:

```spice
MP0 vdd vdd vdd vdd sg13_hv_pmos m=1 w=4.65u l=450.00n ng=1
```

The native gate rectangle is exactly 0.45 × 4.65 µm, local
(41.54,161.62)–(41.99,166.27). Independent native conductor probes connect
the gate to the published M3/M4/M5/TM1 VDD annotations. PNL and ODB bind
each pad's `vdd` to **VDD**, distinct from VSS, IOVDD and IOVSS. The marked
gate is not the EN, SCLK or SDI signal input. This source/native correlation
does not replace unresolved full-chip strict device matching.

The original Metal5/TM1/TM2 cumulative ratios were approximately
27,635 / 37,119 / 37,129, above the protected-net limit 20,000.
`has_diode=1` already; it is a boolean, not scalable credit for adding more
diodes. A synthetic pinned-KLayout deep-mode API control establishes that
`evaluate_nets` evaluates each stage immediately: separate gate ratios
4 and 8/3 become 3 and 3 only after a later bridge. A join made solely on
upper metal cannot retroactively repair the Metal5 failure.

ratio parity. Their outer timeout failed to terminate the container workload;
the exact containers were explicitly stopped at approximately 222 s and
195 s. Subsequent controls put timeout inside the container command.

new connection's load sharing, transient current, IR, EM and lifetime are
unqualified; antenna success is not a current-capacity claim.
