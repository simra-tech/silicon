All source paths in the table are relative to the pinned Academy checkout's
`modules/` directory. Layout bounding boxes include drawing extents and are not
legal G1 placement proposals. A distinct schematic, GDS and extracted subcircuit
being present does not establish their equivalence; that comparison remains
**not run** for both candidates.

The comparator is worth an identical-fixture kickback/offset comparison, but the
Academy source impedance, 6.4 pF input loading, 100 MHz exercise and 0.6 V input
condition cannot be substituted for the G1 hold/divider/source network. The
candidate also needs a defined bias source, complementary-output receiver
mapping, polarity and evaluate/reset timing. Compare the same G1 common-mode
range, finite clock edges, both decision polarities and ±0.1/0.25/0.5/1 mV shunt
guard bands before considering layout integration. G1's PDK and qualified MC
harness must be used. Tutorial histogram figures are not G1 performance evidence.

The OTA has a concrete direct-reuse obstacle: its schematic uses 1.2 V LV
devices, while G1 SENSE operates at 3.3 V and has near-ground inputs and a
resistive feedback/output load. A direct connection to G1's 3.3 V supply is
incompatible with the source testbench/device domain. Resizing/replacing devices
or changing supply/interfaces would be a redesign requiring complete validation.
The source is useful for AC/stability and MC testbench methods; it is not an
immediately reusable G1 SENSE hard macro. The existing G1 OTA is separately
derived from TO_Nov2024/BG, with its changes documented in the block README.

**Decision:** retain both installed G1 blocks for current verification; do not
replace either based on tutorial provenance. Preserve the comparator as the
first controlled A/B candidate. Reference replacement has additional PTAT/bias
interface consequences and was not qualified by this screen. A candidate must
improve a demonstrated failing mechanism and pass the same electrical and
physical acceptance checks before replacement.
