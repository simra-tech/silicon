## Explicit gradient sensitivity

`gradient-20260921.csv` contains 120 **analytical ideal-resistor** cases using
each audited unit position and `R_i/R_unit = 1 + gx(x_i−x0) + gy(y_i−y0)`.
It evaluates both signs of x/y gradients at 1 and 10 ppm/µm, plus zero;
VREF=1.00/1.04 V, common mode −0.1/0/0.3 V and differential 0/50 mV.
These specified perturbations are not physical distributions or PDK simulations.

At zero gradient the ideal gain-20/pedestal equation is recovered to floating
precision (<5e−11 µV input referred). Across the listed cases, absolute
input-referred perturbation reaches about **0.189 µV at 1 ppm/µm x** and
**2.021 µV at 1 ppm/µm y**; at 10 ppm/µm those become **1.884 µV** and
**20.213 µV**. The equation includes R2N/R1N, R2P/R1P and the RD1/RD2 pedestal;
it assumes ideal OTAs and ignores active-device mismatch, thermal behavior and
wire/contact resistance. It therefore cannot pass the calibrated 0.5 mV offset
criterion or predict yield. A separate loaded PDK sensitivity netlist and
frozen-calibration comparison remain **not run**.
