## Native hierarchy/name interface

The inspected integrated GDS has top
`placed_core_NOT_CONNECTED_FULLCHIP`, whereas the source top is
`g1_chip_top`. All 4,904 direct native instances map uniquely to source
masters and transforms; none are unmatched and there are no additional
route-only child instances. Thirty explicit cell aliases are recorded:
`retained_...`, `retained_fullchip_...`, and the BGR/SENSE `_candidate` names.
These agree with the source-preserving copy/refresh implementation.

This result establishes **name/instance placement correspondence**, not an
implicit permission to rename GDS cells or modify a rule deck. The pinned
deck calls its standard `align` before simplification and comparison; actual
hierarchical LVS behavior for this assembled pair remains not run. Any
necessary name-only comparison view must be separately coordinated, preserve
all original identities and bodies, and retain this source-bound reference.
Native connectivity and device recognition are still independent gates.
