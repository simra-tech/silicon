## Tap model interpretation is not a source-syntax repair

The pinned physical tap class treats A/P as primary geometric parameters. Its
combiner sums both; the primitive extractor measures the actual derived tie
region. Independent published raw-layer geometry already proves the LevelUp
tie is a 3.51×0.30 µm rectangle (A1.053/P7.62), while its source A1.051/P4.1
closely describes an equivalent-area square. Rounding cannot explain that large
perimeter difference. The rectangle schematic symbol and `CbTapCalc` use area
and perimeter conductances; the ngspice wrapper consumes the resulting R and
does not use W/L internally. The ring symbol has a separate perimeter expression.

Thus changing A/P while silently retaining electrical R is not a demonstrated
cross-view correction. Nor is substituting an effective square for a native
long tie under the unchanged geometric comparator. The exact pinned code and
independent geometric proof remain in
[the tap applicability audit](../../../io_tap_closure/RESULTS_20260922.md).
No physical/electrical tap model change is made here. A mutually consistent
geometric/electrical tap interpretation remains required.

The exact pinned primitive generator independently confirms this interpretation:
it defines A from W×L and perimeter from 2(W+L), derives R through `CbTapCalc`,
and draws the active tie as that W-by-L rectangle, with the substrate pin over
the same region. The metal/contact dimensions are derived separately; the
generator supplies no equivalent-square conversion for arbitrary guard shapes.
[Pinned ptap1 generator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IHP-GmbH/IHP-Open-PDK/84374023ee8b4b126bebbba67fcbada0a9c0ff0b/ihp-sg13g2/libs.tech/klayout/python/sg13g2_pycell_lib/ihp/ptap1_code.py).

Applying only the existing rectangle conductance expression to the combined
numbers illustrates the consequence: the IOVSS–VSS group gives 3.83235 mΩ from
the source values versus 3.28468 mΩ from the native geometric values. These are
formula illustrations, **not qualified substrate resistances**. They do not
authorize substituting either number into an electrical model. The two global
groups also have different perimeter ratios, ruling out one universal unit or
constant-factor correction. This is not repaired by rounding or reader syntax.

Even an exact supplied electrical R cannot uniquely recover geometric A/P from
this formula: hypothetical 4×4 µm and 2×7 µm rectangular ties have respectively
A/P=16/16 and 14/18. Both give 30.625 Ω under the same rectangle expression.
This is a mathematical non-uniqueness example, not a fabricated or qualified
tap. Therefore inferring new golden geometry from the existing electrical R
would require an independently specified shape, not just algebraic inversion.
