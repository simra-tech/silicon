# BGR conditional full-metal nonlinear DC sensitivity

The two bounded nominal OP experiments completed, but **neither is a qualified
physical parasitic view or an adopted electrical source**. Under the stated
native-M1 and substrate-boundary assumptions, VREF differs materially from the
exact original-source control. Fixed-current routing improvements therefore
do not establish preservation of the nonlinear reference operating point.

All values below are **simulated**, not measured. These are individual DC
solutions, not proofs of unique equilibrium, successful startup or stability.
Canonical source586, calibration and other campaigns were not changed.

The reduced supply current is a changed conditional OP, **not a capacity
credit for power-feed sizing**. No calibration or electrical acceptance was
performed using these values. Node spans and changes in absolute operating
voltage are different quantities; a smaller wire span does not demonstrate
that VREF or the bias network has remained unchanged.

Read-only saved-data analysis uses actual new MOS D/S voltages with the
separately representative-probe-qualified `ctype`/D-S mapping, and the existing
HBT external-current sign mapping. No MOS orientation classification changed.

and 9.153e−14 A (LEF). The inferred MOS/HBT terminal-node residual over 2548
nodes was 1.968e−14 A and 1.792e−14 A, respectively. These residuals are reported
observations, not retrospective electrical-acceptance thresholds or proof of
physical model coverage.

All resistor end/BN currents are deliberately excluded from the complete
external-terminal-current claim at the new split-node bias. Their raw `ibody`
values are retained, but nominal zero-BN inference was not silently extended.

## Limitations and retained failures

Native M1 spreading may overlap VBIC `re` or R3CMC `rc`; physical electrode
reference planes remain unresolved. Body/substrate terminals use selected
lumped guard M1 points. All 399 resistor BN terminals remain ideal global VSS,
without a qualified physical metal attachment. Historical capacitor landing
sites are unqualified; this is DC only. The prior resistor-field CC
completeness failure is unchanged. Thus these numerical results cannot close
full IR, full PEX, transistor safety, temperature accuracy or tape-out approval.

Preparation r1 failed before source generation because the harness compared a
successful audit's descriptive status to the shorter literal `passed`. Its
receipt and worker snapshot are retained. Corrected r2 passed; r3 added full
node-voltage observability without changing either conditional circuit source.
Zero-control r1 failed before ngspice launch because the command supplied the
archive manifest identity to a check expecting the image/config identity.
Corrected invocation r2 used the verified config identity; no source, model,
tolerance or watchdog change was made. Both original failures remain failures.

## Reproduction and evidence

`prepare_sensitivity.py` prepares immutable scenario sources and OP fixtures;
`audit_sensitivity_source.py` independently checks saved-source topology.
`run_sensitivity.py --case zero` executes the control, and `--case kpex` or
`--case lef --zero-control <completed-control>` executes one bounded leaf.
The complete option lists are available through `--help`; preparation, output,
fresh site resource-gate paths and the verified image/config identity are
mandatory. Each run was pinned to CPU0 and one thread; memory was a reservation,
not an enforced rootless-v1 limit. `analyze_sensitivity.py` performs saved-data
attribution without rerunning ngspice.

Portable evidence is under `evidence/conditional-op-20260922-r1/`, including all
three preparation revisions, both control attempts and both scenario runs.
The export manifest records original and exported hashes whenever host-prefix
normalization was necessary. Complete raw metallic networks and physical
geometry remain in the separately committed routing evidence; their exact
hashes are bound in the preparation receipts. No additional leaf or geometry
revision was launched after this sequence.
