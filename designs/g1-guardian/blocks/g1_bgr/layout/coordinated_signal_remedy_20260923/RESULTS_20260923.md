# Bias-route bypass: physical pass, negligible VREF benefit

An isolated additive pbias/pcasc bypass passes geometry, stock DRC/LVS and
source-junction checks. It reduces the targeted voltage spreads, but changes
conditional simulated VREF by less than 0.4 uV. It does **not** resolve the
37.8–46.9 mV conditional VREF deficit and is not adopted into the chip.

## Physical evidence and retained failures

The first three-net screen failed foreign-metal clearance at five proposed
M4 shapes. It generated no candidate. The second screen narrowed the risers
to single columns of four Via4 cuts and deferred the obstructed vb2 escape.
Only pbias and pcasc remain, with 2 um wide M5 parallel routes at local
y=156/159 um and M4 connections to their existing columns.

Build r1 failed its native-cell audit because it compared transient numeric
KLayout cell indices before/after GDS serialization. Read-only comparison
showed the same named children/transforms with renumbered indices. Build r2
changed only that audit to compare child names, transforms, array vectors,
counts and properties; the route ledger did not change. Both failures and
their snapshots remain in the evidence.

Build r2 passed in 9.03 s: exact native local shapes/instances/text, all-layer
native-plus-overlay XOR, nine original pins, all55 source nets, seven star
cuts, and complete intended-net ownership of every added metal/cut face.
Stock main and maximal DRC completed with zero markers in 40.37 s total.
Strict stock LVS passed in 8.10 s: 32 combined devices,24 nets and9 pins,
all Match, no MatchWithWarning promotion. These are stock-combined counts,
not a claim of 1036 independently extracted devices. Independent source336
MOS W/L/AS/AD/PS/PD comparison passed across20 combined groups.
