# Source-held DVBE metal widening

This isolated candidate widens the existing DVBE M5 trunk without changing
source586, its 1036 device records, nine macro ports, device placement,
420 × 354 µm footprint, compact models or stock decks. It is not adopted
into the assembled chip or the qualified electrical source.

The baseline is native GDS `6d86b9d40333958cb8186d486d29db1532a816e302871e11d07e0f1d999cfc04`.
The candidate is `52e0c832f4002a9ec84ea474b50215695ad9e221187e25079ed98b611e533481`.
Only two additive M5 rectangles change: [201,18.67,205,128] µm and
[202.7,127.7,203.3,137.05] µm. No cut, native device or external pin moves.

The matched VREF increase is **17.222935 mV**, not full recovery. The
changed bias increases supply current and other net drops; it is not an
across-the-board routing improvement. All-node voltage extrema were
enumerated directly despite the separately failed exact maximum-principle
test. Candidate maximum metal-interior KCL residual is 7.688e−14 A;
the representative-model-mapping MOS/HBT point residual is 2.545e−14 A.
Complete device-terminal KCL is **not run**, because resistor external
substrate/end-current attribution at the changed bias is unqualified.

The conditional metal fixture uses published native M2 emitter pins for
all 301 HBTs and retains the original resistor M1 attachment points.
Resistor BN remains ideal global VSS; contact/substrate reference planes,
model-overlap de-embedding and complete PolyRes field capacitance remain
unqualified. Historical 329 capacitors remain in the DC-only source view.
No intrinsic model resistance is subtracted, no fitted card is changed,
and no fixed-current result is presented as nonlinear qualification.

The comparison includes all 192 DVBE emitter primitives (eight groups of
24), all 24 reference-emitter primitives and XR16, not merely one
representative per group. PVT, startup, stability, mismatch and electrical
adoption of this candidate are **not run**.
