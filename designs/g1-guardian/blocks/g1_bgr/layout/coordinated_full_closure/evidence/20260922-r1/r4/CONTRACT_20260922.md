their existing fixed coordinates, without source, model, device-card or native
drawing changes. The target is exactly1036 source devices in420×354µm and

Metal5/Via4 are new bank interconnect layers. Six shared resistor/MOS nets
use right-side M5 trunks at403.7+0.75×MOS-net-index µm. Three HBT/MOS nets
(b1b,c2,vbe) cross the inter-bank gap on M4 at130.0/130.7/131.4µm and
join those right-side trunks. HBT dvbe/vbe3/vd1 connect to the corresponding
existing resistor collectors on M5 columns selected by an actual M4 obstacle
query. HBT columns have at least1.1µm center separation. Local Via3/Via4 use
two0.19µm cuts,0.42µm pitch,0.055µm enclosure. Stock decks decide legality.
The existing HBT/R finite star overlap is retained; no additional HBT VSS
connection is added. MOS VSS joins only the resistor general-VSS collector.
