# G1_DUT

State: **simulated, laid out, DRC and LVS clean at block level** (2026-09-18);
**placeable macro `g1_dut_macro` (34 × 34 µm, Metal3 pins, LEF, own fill):
DRC 0, LVS pass, LEF loads in OpenROAD** (2026-09-19). Pad routing is the chip
level's; no PEX.

One `npn13G2` SiGe HBT with all three terminals on analog pads (`HBT_E`,
`HBT_B`, `HBT_C`, pins 22-24) and the substrate on `VSS`. It is the device
that gets measured directly over temperature and dose; the same device model
sits under `G1_BGR` and `G1_T2F`.

## Device

`npn13G2`, `Nx = 1`, default emitter: one 0.07 µm × 0.90 µm window (mask
size, from the model header; the PCell parameters are `we = 0.07u`,
`le = 0.9u`). Available emitter geometries in this PDK:

| PCell / model | Emitter (W × L) | Multiplicity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `npn13G2` | 0.07 × 0.90 µm | `Nx` 1 … 10 emitters in parallel |
| `npn13G2L` | 0.07 × `le`, default 1.0 µm (long-emitter variant) | `Nx` 1 … 4 |
| `npn13G2V` | 0.12 × `le`, default 1.0 µm (high-voltage variant) | `Nx` 1 … 8 |

Model card (`sg13g2_hbt_mod.lib`, VBIC 1.15): TNOM 27 °C, characterised
−40 … +125 °C, valid for IC < 3 mA·Nx, VBE 0.65 … 0.96 V, VCE 0.4 … 2.0 V.
Forward ideality `nf = 1.018` with `tnf = 1.5e-4 /K`.

## Simulated

Decks are generated by `sim/gen_decks.py` and run by `sim/run_all.sh`
(inside `flow/run.sh`, working directory `sim/`). Model include line exactly as
used, `<corner>` ∈ {`hbt_typ`, `hbt_bcs`, `hbt_wcs`}:

```
.lib /foss/pdks/ihp-sg13g2/libs.tech/ngspice/models/cornerHBT.lib <corner>
XQ1 c b e 0 npn13G2 Nx=1
```

(`/foss/pdks` is `PDK_ROOT` inside the pinned container.) Per corner and
temperature (−40, 27, 85, 125 °C inside the model range; 150, 175 and
−196 °C **model extrapolated**, outside the card's characterised range):

- forward Gummel at VCB = 0 (a 0 V source between base and collector), VBE
  0.30 … 1.00 V in 1 mV steps: IC, IB, β → `sim/decks/<corner>_T<T>C_gummel.dat`
- output curves IC(VCE), VCE 0 … 1.6 V, IB = 1 … 8 µA in 1 µA steps →
  `sim/decks/<corner>_T<T>C_output.dat`
- `.meas`: VBE at IC = 0.1, 1, 8, 10 µA; IB and β at 1 and 10 µA; β at 100 µA;
  peak β → `sim/logs/<corner>_T<T>C_gummel.log`

Reading of the ΔVBE column, which is what `G1_T2F` will build on:

- ΔVBE(8:1) is linear in T with the ideal slope to within the ideality factor:
  the model's `nf = 1.018` at 27 °C plus `tnf` gives 1.040 at 175 °C, which
  explains most of the +2.7 % → +5.4 % excess over (kT/q) ln 8. The excess is
  almost corner-independent (±0.1 %), i.e. a fixed gain error that a two-point
  calibration removes.
- VBE(1 µA) falls 1.42 mV/K between −40 and 125 °C (typ). β rises towards cold
  and the −196 °C values (β > 10⁵) are the VBIC temperature law extrapolated
  far outside its fit range; they are reported only to bound what the bench
  should not be surprised by, not as a prediction.
- Corners move VBE(1 µA) by −6 / +8 mV at 27 °C and β by ×1.8 / ×0.57 (bcs / wcs).

Log hygiene: ngspice printed "singular matrix" warnings in seven runs (six
output-curve decks at the VCE = 0 start point, one Gummel deck at 125 °C typ);
gmin stepping recovered and every `.meas` value is present.

## Laid out

`layout/g1_dut_layout.py` (KLayout batch Python, run in the container) writes
`layout/g1_dut.gds`, top cell `g1_dut`, 8.9 × 11 µm:

- one `npn13G2` PCell instance, `Nx = 1`, from the PDK library `SG13_dev`
  (instantiated with `add_pcell_variant`, not redrawn);
- the PCell's own p+ substrate ring (Activ + pSD, 0.5 µm wide) contacted with a
  Cont ring at 0.34 µm pitch and a 0.26 µm Metal1 ring, labelled `VSS`
  (Metal1 text layer 8/25, pin 8/2);
- terminals on Metal2 so the Metal1 ring stays closed: collector and base via
  Via1 (4 each, on the PCell's Metal1 C and B pads widened to 0.29/0.33 µm for
  the 0.05 µm Via1 enclosure), emitter directly from the PCell's Metal2 pad;
  0.30 µm Metal2 strips to 1 × 1 µm Metal2 pads labelled `HBT_C` (north),
  `HBT_B` (south), `HBT_E` (east) on layer 10/25 with pin shapes on 10/2.

One iteration was needed: the first base Metal2 pad sat 0.235 µm from the
emitter Metal2 pad; `M2.e` asks 0.24 µm between wide parallel Metal2 lines.

## Macro for integration: `g1_dut_macro`

`layout/g1_dut_macro.py` (helpers in `../g1_dose/layout/macro_common.py`, run
in the container) wraps the cell of record `g1_dut` into the placeable macro
**`g1_dut_macro`, 34 × 34 µm, origin (0, 0)**, and writes
`layout/g1_dut_macro.gds` (flat, one cell), `layout/g1_dut_macro.lef`,
`layout/g1_dut_macro.vh` (black box) and `layout/lef_check.tcl`, following
`../g1_padring/INTEGRATION.md` and `../g1_bgr/layout/g1_bgr.lef`.

| Pin | LEF | Metal3 RECT (µm) | Connection inside |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `HBT_B` | INOUT, SIGNAL | west edge, `0 11.85 1.5 12.15` | Via2 on the base Metal2 pad (south of the device), Metal3 west |
| `HBT_C` | INOUT, SIGNAL | west edge, `0 21.85 1.5 22.15` | Via2 on the collector Metal2 pad (north), Metal3 west |
| `HBT_E` | INOUT, SIGNAL | west edge, `0 23.35 1.5 23.65` | Via2 on the emitter Metal2 pad (east), Metal3 north over the cell, then west |
| `vss` | INOUT, GROUND | south bar, `0 0 34 2` | Metal1 patch + Via1 + Metal2 + Via2 on the p+ ring Metal1 (SE corner), Metal3 south |

Signal pins are 1.5 × 0.30 µm Metal3 stubs on the west edge (pads 22-24 are
on the west side), labels on 30/25, pin shapes on 30/2. OBS: Metal1 and
Metal2 over the whole macro, Metal3 from (1.8, 2.6) to (34, 34); nothing on
Metal4 and above. prBoundary 189/4 = the macro box. The device cell sits at
(15.925, 17.0), bbox (12.575, 11.5)-(21.425, 22.5). OpenROAD loads the LEF
with the PDK tech LEF (`reports/lef_check_g1_dut_macro.log`: `MASTER
g1_dut_macro BLOCK 34.0 x 34.0 um`, four pins, three OBS layers).

**Size.** 30 × 30 µm was tried first: with the 5 µm no-fill margin around the
8.85 × 11 µm device cell the remaining bands are 3.3-4.4 µm, and the PDK
filler places a fill cell only where its whole tile (cell + spacing, lattice
anchored at the origin: 2.5 µm Metal1/2, 3 µm Metal3, 5 µm Activ) fits, so
that macro received Metal1/Metal2 fill only. 34 × 34 is the smallest size
that fills all five obstructed layers: 6 Activ (3.4 × 3.4), 6 GatPoly (5 ×
1.4), 72 Metal1, 72 Metal2 and 12 Metal3 (1 × 1) shapes.

**Fill.** No-fill shapes on all fill layers plus `NoMetFiller` (the set the
`NoFillerStack` PCell writes) cover the device cell with a 5 µm margin,
(7.575, 6.5)-(26.425, 27.5), plus Metal3 no-fill over the pin-access bands
(x < 2.3, y < 3.1). The PDK filler runs on a temporary copy with an `EdgeSeal`
frame (it fills only `EdgeSeal` holes; hole = macro box inset 1.2 µm) and
`-rd no_topmetal` (`reports/filler_g1_dut_macro.log`); the datatype-22 fill on
Activ, GatPoly, Metal1, Metal2, Metal3 is copied back flattened. Metal4/5 and
TopMetal carry no macro fill: the chip filler fills them over the footprint
and honours the no-fill shapes (the `g1_dose` README has the reasoning).

**Fill and LVS.** The LVS deck joins the fill datatypes into its connectivity
layers rather than ignoring them (`layers_definitions.lvs`); fill islands are
floating nets that the simplification purges. The LVS of the filled macro
passes with the same single `npn13G2 we=70n le=900n Nx=1` as the unfilled
cell (`reports/lvs_macro/g1_dut_macro_extracted.cir`).

**PDK finding: the DRC deck recognises the HBT through its pin shapes.** The
first flat macro removed all Metal2.pin (10/2) shapes and rule `nSDB.e`
("nSD:block and Cont do not overlap") fired on the PCell's own base contact.
Cause (`sg13g2_maximal.drc`): the npn13G2 device region is derived as
`TRANS interacting with TEXT "npn13G2"` *covering* `Metal2.pin ∩ TRANS
interacting with TEXT "E"`, i.e. from the PCell's emitter Metal2.pin shape and
its 63/0 texts; without them the deck no longer knows the nSD.block belongs to
an HBT and applies the plain contact rule. Any flattening, pin-datatype
stripping or text stripping of a design with `npn13G2` PCells must keep the
10/2 shapes and the 63/0 texts of the PCell, or DRC fails. The macro keeps
them (only its own Metal1/Metal2 labels on 8/25 and 10/25 are dropped).

## Unverified

- Pad routing: the macro's Metal3 pins end at the macro edge; the route to
  pins 22-24 is the chip level's, and the analog pad ESD structures are not
  drawn here. The pad's ESD diode leakage sets the lowest usable device
  current and is not yet simulated (PLAN R7).
- Chip-level PDN strap onto the 34 µm `vss` bar (TopMetal1 stripes every
  75.6 µm may not cross it; a jog may be needed, see `INTEGRATION.md`). Pin
  stubs are not on the Metal3 track grid, same as the other analog macros.
- Density inside the macro is not checked at block level.
- No ESD protection beyond what the IO cell provides.
- Model extrapolation at 150, 175 and −196 °C; nothing below −40 °C or above
  125 °C is a prediction.
- Self-heating: the model has `selft = 1`; the Gummel sweep reaches IC ≈ 1 mA at
  VBE = 1 V, where self-heating in the model is active but not separately checked.
- Inverse-mode Gummel (E and C swapped), planned as a dose observable, is not
  simulated; the bench plan covers it.
- PEX and post-layout simulation: not run.
