# g1_ls — level shifter cells for the G1 analog wrapper

State: **one cell, `g1_ls_up` (1.2 V → 3.3 V): schematic simulated over
corners, laid out, DRC clean (full rule set), LVS clean.** No PEX, no
post-layout simulation, no silicon. Owner: the digital track (`G1_CTRL`); the
cells are placed by the analog wrapper / integration owner (`PLAN.md` D8).

## Why and how many

The PDK has no standalone level shifter (`PLAN.md` section 3); the shifters
inside the IO pads are not usable for internal signals. Every signal between
the digital macro and the analog blocks is 1.2 V logic (`../g1_trip/INTERFACE.md`
"Domains", `../layout/PINS.md`), except the three static sensor/bandgap control
bits of `../g1_t2f/INTERFACE.md`, which are 3.3 V at the analog pins:

| Digital pin | Analog pin (3.3 V) | Instance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `t2f_en` | `g1_t2f.en` | `g1_ls_up` #1 |
| `t2f_mode` | `g1_t2f.mode` | `g1_ls_up` #2 |
| `bgr_r4` | `g1_bgr.r4` | `g1_ls_up` #3 |

**Instance count: 3 × `g1_ls_up`.** No 3.3 V → 1.2 V shifter is needed by any
interface signal: `tripped` is level-shifted to 1.2 V inside G1_GATE
(`../g1_trip/INTERFACE.md`; G1_GATE carries its own up/down shifters,
`../g1_gate/sim/netlist/g1_lvlup.spice`, `g1_lvldn.spice`), `en` is the pad's
1.2 V `p2c` output, `cmp_soft`/`cmp_hard`/`osc_clk` come from 1.2 V blocks and
`TEMP_OUT` is driven by the 1.2 V `g1_t2f.fout` directly into the output pad.
All three shifted signals are static register bits (no timing requirement).

## Circuit

`xschem/g1_ls_up.sch` (generated by `xschem/gen_ls.py`, symbol `g1_ls_up.sym`),
ports `in out vdd vdda vss`. Structure of the PDK IO-cell shifter
`sg13g2_LevelUp` (`libs.ref/sg13g2_io/spice/sg13g2_io.spi`), re-sized: a 1.2 V
inverter (`in` → `inb`) drives two thick-oxide NMOS pull-downs; a
cross-coupled thick-oxide PMOS pair on `vdda` holds the complementary nodes
`n`/`nb`; a thick-oxide inverter from `nb` gives the full-swing output
(`out` follows `in`). No DC path in either state. The pull-downs are 3.8 µm
(the IO cell uses 1.9 µm) so the 1.08 V-driven NMOS wins the contention against
the keeper at `vdda` = 3.63 V, `mos_ss`, −40 °C.

| Device | Type | W / L (µm) | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MPI / MNI | `sg13_lv_pmos` / `sg13_lv_nmos` | 2 / 0.13, 1 / 0.13 | 1.2 V inverter |
| MN1 / MN2 | `sg13_hv_nmos` | 3.8 / 0.45 | pull-downs of `nb` (gate `in`) and `n` (gate `inb`) |
| MP1 / MP2 | `sg13_hv_pmos` | 0.3 / 0.45 | cross-coupled keeper on `vdda` |
| MPO / MNO | `sg13_hv_pmos` / `sg13_hv_nmos` | 3.9 / 0.45, 1.9 / 0.45 | output inverter |

Netlists: `sim/netlist/g1_ls_up.spice` (xschem output, `.subckt`, for ngspice)
and `sim/netlist/g1_ls_up.cdl` (the same netlist with the MOS devices as `M`
elements for the KLayout LVS reader; produced by `xschem/netlist.sh` from the
schematic, nothing edited by hand).

## Simulated (schematic level, no parasitics)

ngspice 46 in the pinned container (PDK commit `8437402…`, PSP103 OSDI models,
`cornerMOSlv.lib` / `cornerMOShv.lib`). Run from the repository root:

```
G1_WORKDIR=designs/g1-guardian/blocks/g1_ctrl/ls/sim flow/run.sh bash run_ls.sh all
```

Results table `sim/results_ls.txt` (81 + 6 lines), raw measurements
`sim/logs/sweep.log`, `sim/logs/pwr.log` (0 ngspice errors).

**Corner sweep** (`sim/tb_ls.cir`): `mos_tt/ff/ss` × `vdd` 1.08/1.2/1.32 V ×
`vdda` 2.97/3.3/3.63 V × −40/27/175 °C = 81 points; `in` steps 0 → `vdd` with
1 ns edges, 20 fF on `out`; delays 50 % `in` → 50 % `out`.

| Quantity (simulated) | Nominal `mos_tt` 1.2 V / 3.3 V 27 °C | Range over the 81 points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rise delay `tr` | 630 ps | 440 ps (`ff` 1.32/2.97 V −40 °C) … 1067 ps (`ss` 1.08/3.63 V 175 °C) |
| fall delay `tf` | 789 ps | 527 ps (`ff` 1.32/3.63 V −40 °C) … 1393 ps (`ss` 1.08/2.97 V 175 °C) |
| output levels | 3.30 V / 0.0 V | full swing at every point (0 points outside 10–90 % of `vdda`) |
| static `vdda` current, either state | 11 pA | ≤ 10.3 nA (`ff` 175 °C) |
| static `vdd` current, either state | ≤ 81 pA | ≤ 116 nA (`ff` 175 °C, LV inverter leakage) |
| peak `vdda` current during the rising edge | 606 µA | 271 µA (`ss` 1.08/2.97 V 175 °C) … 1031 µA (`ff` 1.32/3.63 V −40 °C) |

**Power-up** (`sim/tb_ls_pwr.cir`, 27 °C, `mos_tt/ss/ff`): `vdda` ramped 1 µs
before `vdd` (order A) or after it (order D), `in` low during the ramps, then
one 1.2 V pulse on `in`, then `vdd` removed while `vdda` stays. In all six
cases `out` stays at 0 V (< 10 µV transient, nV static) with either rail alone
and with both rails, follows the pulse to 3.3 V, and stays low with `vdd`
absent (`vdda` current 12–22 pA). The cell therefore does not produce a false
3.3 V level in any supply sequence, which matters because `t2f_en` is the
sensor's enable and `bgr_r4` changes the reference.

−196 °C (the `PLAN.md` step-2 extrapolation run): not run for this cell.

## Laid out

`layout/g1_ls_up.py` (KLayout 0.30.9, Python API) builds the cell from the PDK
MOS PCells (`SG13_dev`: `nmos`, `pmos`, `nmosHV`, `pmosHV`) plus hand-drawn
well and substrate ties, Metal1/Metal2 wiring, one `ThickGateOx` rectangle over
the 3.3 V part, pin labels and the placement boundary (`prBoundary` 189/4,
0–9.2 × 0–11.7 µm, added after the chip dry run found it missing), and writes
`layout/g1_ls_up.gds`, `layout/g1_ls_up.lef` and `layout/g1_ls_up.vh`. Run
from the repository root:

```
G1_WORKDIR=designs/g1-guardian/blocks/g1_ctrl/ls/layout flow/run.sh klayout -b -r g1_ls_up.py
```

### Placement in the chip (as done by the ring owner, D14 dry run)

The three cells are placed as tiny `MACROS` instances (`i_core.u_ls_r4`,
`u_ls_mode`, `u_ls_en` at x = 546 / 566 / 586, y = 860 µm, 20 µm pitch, next
to `g1_t2f`; `../../g1_padring/INTEGRATION.md`, `reports/dryrun-1350/`), and
their `vdd` / `vdda` / `vss` Metal1 rails are strapped by the ring owner's
`analog_straps.tcl` (`via1_2` + Metal2 jog to the nearest TopMetal1 stripe;
"option b"). No objection from this side: `vdd` is the 1.2 V core rail, `vdda`
the 3.3 V analog supply `VDDA` (pin 7 after D14), `vss` the common ground; the
cell is not a standard-cell-row cell (no `sg13g2` site, 11.7 µm tall), so a
macro placement is the right kind, and the Metal2 windows in the LEF over the
three rails exist for exactly such straps. Two conditions, both met by the
20 µm pitch: instances must stay ≥ 1.2 µm apart (the `vdd` well ends 0.29 µm
inside the left edge and the `vdda` well 0.35 µm inside the right edge; two
abutted cells would put wells on different supplies 0.64 µm apart against
NW.b1 = 1.8 µm — abutment was **not** checked and is not DRC-clean), and the
rails are not connected by abutment, so every instance needs its own three
straps. The chip-level DRC of the dry run (0 markers, hard rules) covers the
placed cells and their straps; the cell's own DRC/LVS below is unchanged by
the prBoundary addition.

The first DRC pass of the generated layout had 9 markers (two `Cnt.b`, one
`Cnt.c` in the hand-drawn ties, six `M2.a` notches where a horizontal and a
vertical Metal2 wire met without overlapping at the corner); all were fixed in
the generator (`git log` of `layout/g1_ls_up.py`), none by editing the GDS.

## Unverified

capacitances set the delay and were not extracted); the cell's delay into the
real `g1_t2f.en`/`mode` and `g1_bgr.r4` loads (simulated with 20 fF); mismatch
statistics of the keeper/pull-down contention; behaviour below −40 °C;
anything on silicon.
