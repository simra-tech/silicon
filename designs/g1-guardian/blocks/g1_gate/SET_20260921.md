# Exploratory GATE latch current-pulse injection

**Simulated**, ngspice46, pinned IHP commit84374023ee8b4b126bebbba67fcbada0a9c0ff0b; unchanged C-PEX model. `sim/run_set_campaign.py --full --image-id sha256:5fd78498e578c6e9ec10828c248ca3790fc88250ff6caf545521b29e448ea3c0` through the pinned `flow/run.sh` reproduces24cases. Evidence `sim/campaigns/set_20260921T152635Z_9cb49d9d`; preceding100fC8casepilot `set_20260921T152225Z_a2af03f0`. Every solver run completes with saved finite vectors and established initial state.

The fixture injects a1ns area-equivalent trapezoid,10ps rise/fall, into each internal cross-coupled latch node `n_34`/`n_35`, both signs,10/100/1000fC, from armed and previously tripped states. It uses nominal rails/temperature and20fF loads on the three core logic outputs. It excludes the real output pad/FET and any digital reassertion of the trip signal. Charge is an imposed electrical stimulus, **not particle LET, collected-charge prediction, or radiation qualification**.

At10and100fC all16cases retain/recover the intended final latch state. At1000fC four ofeight cases change the final state: positive injection atn_34 or negative atn_35 falsely trips the armed latch; negative atn_34 or positive atn_35 rearms the tripped latch. These dangerous rearm observations must not be reported as safety passes just because the simulator completed. The other four retain the intended final state.

The largest pulses drive nodes outside ordinary rail bounds (observed minimum−0.812V and maximum4.201V in changed-state cases), so the PDK's validity for these excursions is not established. No critical charge or upset cross section is extracted from this coarse electrical sweep. Not run: pulse-shape/width/PVT refinement, actual pad/FET/digital-feedback recovery, comparator/reference-bias-node SET, correlated physical strikes, irradiation, or statistical radiation rates.

The four1pC tripped-state cases were repeated with `--state tripped --charge-fc 1000 --hold-trip`, keeping the digital trip input asserted (`set_20260921T154021Z_b2a492d7`). All four complete and recover the tripped final state. Negative injection atn_34 nevertheless produces a1.246V transient at the core gate output; the other three peaks remain below3.6mV. Final-state recovery therefore does not establish absence of a gate glitch. This repeat uses an ideal held trip input, not the actual RTL feedback path; output-pad/FET glitch propagation remains not run.

A nominal5V/1A real-FET+detailed-pad repeat of the negative1pC n_34 pulse, with digital trip held, completes (`sim/campaigns/fet_20260921T155901Z_59c83141`): post-injection GATE peak111µV,VGS63µV,load5.07nA. This uses the original loose pad solver settings. Its tight-tolerance/100ps-step repeat (`fet_20260921T160012Z_2515a52c`) fails numerically, so glitch filtering is not numerically qualified. No radiation-rate or full-system recovery claim follows.
