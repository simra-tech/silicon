# G1_GATE — trip latch, level shifters and GATE/FAULT_N pad drive

Current verification status (2026-09-22): **not release-qualified**. The original
IO-first/missing-core unsafe-high failure remains open; a10kΩ pull-down does not
fix it. The isolated IO-powered buffer candidate is unadopted: nominal EN-low
passes do not qualify arming, real-FET loading or protection. Its unchanged-pad
numerical reduction still fails to complete, including the fresh bounded200pA
diagnostic. See [current diagnostics](sim/candidates/io_buffer/DIAGNOSTICS_20260922.md).
Historical completed checks below retain their stated scope and do not waive
these open gates or establish physical measurements.

State: **schematic frozen, simulated at schematic level with the PDK IO-cell SPICE models
(function, timing into 5 nF, power-up with supply ramps in both orders, corners); layout generated,
DRC-clean (full rule set) and LVS-clean (2026-09-19); kpex 2.5D capacitance extraction and
post-layout simulation done; macro fill added, DRC/LVS repeated clean on the filled GDS (see
"Laid out" and "Post-layout" below).**

## What it is

A reset-dominant SR latch built from thick-oxide (`sg13_hv_*`) NOR gates in the 3.3 V domain
holds the trip state. Thick-oxide gates with 3.3 V swing were chosen for the one node whose
upset would drop the load: their critical charge is far above that of 1.2 V core logic (the
digital macro is TMR-protected separately; this latch is not, so the device choice is its
protection). All ports are 1.2 V signals (`vdd`), the cell also takes `vdda` (3.3 V):

```
set   = trip_d | (hard_cmp & fast_en)          (LV AND, then g1_lvlup to 3.3 V)
reset = !en_core | clr_d                       (g1_lvlup to 3.3 V)          reset wins over set
tripped_h = NOR(reset, qb) ; qb = NOR(set, tripped_h)
gate_h    = en_h & !tripped_h                  -> g1_lvldn -> gate_core  (to sg13g2_IOPadOut30mA c2p)
fault_h   = !tripped_h                         -> g1_lvldn -> fault_core (to sg13g2_IOPadOut4mA  c2p)
tripped   = g1_lvldn(tripped_h)                -> digital macro
```

- `g1_lvlup` (1.2 → 3.3 V): LV inverter + two `sg13_hv_nmos` 1.9/0.45 pull-downs + cross-coupled
  `sg13_hv_pmos` 0.3/0.45 + HV inverter; the structure and sizes of the PDK IO `sg13g2_LevelUp` cell.
- `g1_lvldn` (3.3 → 1.2 V): two `sg13_hv_nmos` 2/0.45 pull-downs driven by the signal and its
  complement, cross-coupled `sg13_lv_pmos` 1/0.13 load on `vdd`; the HV drains never exceed 1.2 V.
- The pads themselves (`sg13g2_IOPadOut30mA`, `sg13g2_IOPadOut4mA`, `sg13g2_IOPadIn` for `EN`) are
  PDK cells in the ring and are only instantiated in the testbench (`libs.ref/sg13g2_io/spice/sg13g2_io.spi`).
- Power-up: `en_core` = 0 forces `gate_core` = 0 through the reset and the AND; the latch state is
  irrelevant. What the `GATE` pad does while the 1.2 V core is still absent is decided by the pad's
  own core-powered level shifter, not by this block: see the power-up results below.

Schematics generated by `schematic/gen_gate.py` (cells `g1_lv_inv`, `g1_lv_nand2`, `g1_hv_inv`,
`g1_hv_nor2`, `g1_lvlup`, `g1_lvldn`, top `g1_gate`), netlisted by `schematic/netlist.sh`.

The 5 nF discharge is set by the pad (≈ 30 mA class) and the 10 Ω series resistor, not by the latch;
the whole hard fast path from comparator output to `GATE` < 1 V is 0.3–0.4 µs, against the 10 µs
target. The ss / 3.0 V / 1.08 V / 175 °C run stops with "timestep too small" at the first `EN` edge
**with the IO-pad model**, also with looser tolerances (`gate_mos_ss_3.0V_1.08V_175C_loose.log`); the
same corner with the two pads replaced by 1 Ω links (`run_gate.sh one ... nopad`,
`gate_mos_ss_3.0V_1.08V_175C_nopad.log`) passes the full state table with 2.5 ns (`hard_cmp` →
`gate_core`) and 3.1 ns (`trip_d` → `gate_core`), so the block's own logic and level shifters work at
that corner; what is unverified there is the pad model's discharge time (extrapolate ≈ 1.3× the tt
number from the 3.0 V rail). The same pad-free check at ff / 3.6 V / 1.32 V / −40 °C gives 1.1 ns and
2.3 ns and the full state table (`gate_mos_ff_3.6V_1.32V_-40C_nopad.log`).

Power-up (`sim/results_gate_pwr.txt`, tt 27 °C, `EN` pad held low until 9 µs):

| Order | External pull-down on GATE | `GATE` max while `EN` = 0 | `gate_core` max | `GATE` after `EN` high |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: `IOVDD` up first (1–3 µs), `VDD` later (5–7 µs) | none | **3.30 V** | 0.04 V | 3.30 V |
| A | 10 kΩ | **3.29 V** | 0.04 V | 3.29 V |
| B: `VDD` up first, `IOVDD` later | none | 0.03 mV | 0.60 V (internal glitch, not on the pad) | run aborts at 0.8 µs (pad model, GATE floating without `IOVDD`) — **not converged** |
| B | 10 kΩ | 0.03 mV | 0.60 V | 3.29 V |

**Finding:** with `IOVDD` present and `VDD` absent the `sg13g2_IOPadOut30mA` output goes **high**
although its core input is low — the pad's own core-powered level shifter (`sg13g2_LevelUp`, a
cross-coupled PMOS latch) has no defined state without `VDD`, and the 30 mA driver overrides a 10 kΩ
pull-down. This block cannot fix that; it is a property of the IO cell. The board must bring `VDD`
(1.2 V) up before or together with `IOVDD` (3.3 V), or the external FET may be on until the core rail
arrives. Once `VDD` is up (order B) `GATE` stays low until `EN` is released, as required. This is the
evidence for plan risk R8 and is repeated in `../g1_trip/INTERFACE.md`.


## Laid out

Macro `g1_gate`: **130.68 × 50.65 µm** (boundary on 189/4), generated by `layout/g1_gate_layout.py`
(KLayout 0.30.9 batch Python in the `flow/run.sh` container; PDK PCells `pmos`, `nmos`, `pmosHV`,
`nmosHV`; drawing helpers shared with G1_SENSE, `../g1_sense/layout/g1_layout_lib.py`). Outputs
`layout/g1_gate.gds` (one flat cell `g1_gate`, 78 transistors) and `layout/g1_gate.lef`.
Regenerate: `G1_WORKDIR=designs/g1-guardian/blocks/g1_gate/layout flow/run.sh klayout -b -r g1_gate_layout.py`, then `G1_WORKDIR=designs/g1-guardian/blocks/g1_gate/layout flow/run.sh bash ../../g1_sense/layout/fill.sh g1_gate` for the filled integration file.

Two CMOS rows and a routing band, south to north:

| y (µm) | content |
| --- | --- |
| 1.5 – 4.5 | `vss` bar (Metal3, full width) |
| 6.7 – 13.3 | 1.2 V row: p+ tie strip, vss rail, `nmos` row (Activ 8.5–10.5), shared poly gates with one contact each, `pmos` row (11.3–13.3) in its own NWell (`vdd`), vdd rail, n+ tie strip |

| 46.15 – 49.15 | `vdda` bar (Metal3, full width) |

Cells west to east — 1.2 V row: XNA (nand2), XIA (inv), the four `g1_lvlup` input inverters, the
three `g1_lvldn` cross-coupled `pmos` pairs; 3.3 V row: XU0…XU3 (`g1_lvlup`: 2-finger `nmosHV`
pull-downs, the two `pmosHV` 0.3/0.45 of the cross-coupled pair placed west and east of them so
that every device port has its own x, output inverter), XN1, XI1, XI2, XN2, XI3, XL1, XL2 (the
latch), XG, XGI, the three `g1_lvldn` `nmosHV` pull-down pairs. The 1.2 V cells are placed under
their 3.3 V partners but slid east until none of their ports is within 0.55 µm of a 3.3 V port.
Every device port is a Metal2 pad (drain: via pad on the source/drain strip inside the Activ;
gate: contact on the poly bridge between the NMOS and PMOS fingers, or an individual gate pad,
with a Metal2 stub into the 2 µm inter-cell gap); Metal3 verticals join the ports to their net's
track. The 3.3 V NOR2 devices are single 7.8 µm fingers (schematic `ng = 1`); all widths and
lengths are the schematic's. No dummies (digital cells). The LVS deck flattens the schematic
hierarchy (`g1_lv_inv` … `g1_lvldn`) against the flat layout.

## Unverified

- Single-event upset of the latch (no charge-injection simulation; the argument is device choice only).
- Behaviour with a real FET model on `GATE` (a 5 nF capacitor stands in for the gate charge).
- `FAULT_N` external pull-up/open-drain option: the pad is a push-pull 4 mA output as specified.
- Post-layout at ff/3.6 V/−40 °C with the pad model (stops at 3.4 µs, see above) and at ss/175 °C with
  the pad model (never converged, schematic either); the pad-free runs cover the block's own logic.
- Wiring resistance (kpex RC mode unusable at this version; estimated negligible, see Post-layout).
- Power-up simulation was not repeated on the post-layout netlist (the finding is an IO-cell property).
- Density and antenna (chip level); the block uses nothing above Metal3.
