## Candidate and assumptions

[TI CSD16340Q3](https://www.ti.com/product/CSD16340Q3) is a25 V N-channel
characterization candidate with resistance specified at VGS2.5 V. The public
[unencrypted PSpice archive](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/sllm127b) supplies its
transient model. The model is unchanged, SHA-256
`c5572438f1a79c8e9f48cfcf5a8d152daafdad8e8ec26ac30f807f0211edf2df`.
It remains in the ignored build tree; `vendor/fetch_model.py` retrieves and
checks it. Preserve the vendor's notices. This is not a board BOM selection or
manufacturer guarantee of simulated performance.

The fixture includes the actual GATE block C-PEX and30mA output pad,10 Ω gate
resistor,10 kΩ gate-source pull-down,25 mΩ source shunt and10 mΩ return. EN and
trip are driven at the core interface. Bus voltage, R-L load, and the explicitly
specified flyback diode are **assumed fixture parameters**, not extracted board
parasitics or a selected diode. Driver voltage is3.3 V; core1.2 V; tt,27 °C.
There is no electrothermal/SOA or complete integrated threshold qualification.

Ngspice46 PSpice compatibility is confined to the external model with `.incpslt`.
Four unchanged vendor parameter constants are repeated in the fixture because
its legacy `.PARAM name value` declarations were not resolved. No IHP model
card or vendor model is edited. The resulting model still needs an independent
DC/gate-charge consistency check against its datasheet before board adoption.

## Isolated vendor-model checks

`vendor/validate_model.py` compares the unchanged model under ngspice46 with the25°C conditions in [TI SLPS247E, page3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/csd16340q3.pdf). Corrected campaign `vendorcheck_20260921T155807Z_2c691615` completes all six checks. Simulated on-resistance at20A is6.020/4.288/3.829mΩ for2.5/4.5/8V gate bias; threshold at250µA with drain tied to gate is0.8609V; body-diode forward drop at20A is0.8103V. A constant1mA gate-charge fixture with20A load and12.5V drain clamp gives6.749nC to4.5V. Each is within4% of the corresponding datasheet typical value; the predeclared approximate parser-check tolerance was20%, not a production-device tolerance.

The original charge fixture (`vendorcheck_20260921T155119Z_d2089bcd`) failed at4.541nC. Inspection showed its1TΩ gate return allowed an initial gate voltage1.703V and drain2.735V, violating the intended initial bias. A gate-reset switch now establishes the off condition before charging, and explicit initial gate/drain checks reject invalid fixtures. The failed original run remains preserved. This correction does not alter the vendor model or earlier driven-gate circuit fixtures.

Tight solver tolerances also fail the real-FET core-first power fixture (`fet_20260921T155717Z_5350dfac`); that sequence is still not qualified with the real-FET model.

## Coupled-model isolation and split supply observations

`vendor/replay_gate.py` removes the IHP driver and replays either observed terminal VGS or pad voltage into the original22Ω/10kΩ gate network. It keeps the unchanged vendor FET and the5V/1.25Ω/100nH load/clamp. Both125°C and27°C finite-interval VGS replays complete8µs (`vendor_replay_20260921T165853Z_0ad403df`, `...165854Z_a0ff8b63`), as do pad-voltage replays (`...170312Z_377a686b`, `...170313Z_56a7e89a`). These are numerical isolation tests, not replacement electrical acceptance. The final observed voltage is held after the failed source waveform ends.

Two initial unresampled replays (`...165718Z_303ae0d0`, `...165719Z_8ca954d2`) fail at the source waveform's collapsed timesteps (minimum2.34e−19s). Replays now resample at100ps; maximum VGS reconstruction difference is0.448mV. Original failures remain retained. Removing source impedance or imposing pad voltage changes circuit feedback; these completed isolated runs do not close the coupled fixture.

The coupled slow/hot fixture also fails with first-order Gear (`fet_20260921T170038Z_48843454`,0.208468µs) and with100ps maximum timestep/second-order Gear (`...170040Z_d834dba2`,0.209019µs), both at `xfet.10`. Neither numerical change is adopted as a remedy.

Nominal4A repeat `fet_20260921T165316Z_0f0cfcb4` additionally saves combined source currents. Repeat `...165800Z_38f6131f` inserts zero-volt probes separating core and IO-pad supply currents, retaining the same common upstream0.5Ω/1nF fixture. Gate/current delays differ by less than6e−16s; turnoff energy differs by2.9e−8 relative. `split_power_wave.tsv` is suitable for current-allocation characterization, not independent-rail or assembled-impedance validation. The armed~315µA IO current largely supplies the external10kΩ pulldown; rail input power is not all on-die heat. See the separate V18 audit for integration residuals and limitations.
