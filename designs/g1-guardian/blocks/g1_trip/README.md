# G1_TRIP — threshold comparators and 8-bit threshold DACs (1.2 V)

State: **schematic frozen and simulated (nominal; comparator over temperature/corners; DAC over
resistor corners and temperature; mismatch Monte Carlo on comparator offset and DAC linearity;
kickback with the real G1_SENSE source); laid out as the macro `g1_trip` (229 × 207 µm), DRC clean
with the full PDK rule set and LVS clean against the frozen netlist (2026-09-19); PEX with kpex
2.5D (capacitances) and post-layout runs passed: comparator decisions right down to 1 mV overdrive
in 0.86 ns (tt) / 1.78 ns (ss, 1.08 V, −40 °C), DAC INL/DNL ≤ 0.002 LSB, settling ≤ 0.19 µs; the
comparator layout was revised once for parasitic symmetry after the first extraction (see
"Post-layout"). Chip-integration revision of the same day: macro filled with the PDK filler
(no-fill over the matched structures), `prBoundary` 189/4 added, antenna deck clean, LEF Metal4/5
obstructions leave the supply-bar zones free for the chip PDN.** Interface to the digital macro:
`INTERFACE.md`.

## What it is

```
isense_cmp = ISENSE / 2                       (g1_cond: 5+5 units of the G1_SENSE 10 kΩ rppd, 1 pF cmim hold)
V_DAC(code) = VREF × (255 + code) / 530        (g1_dac8: 0.5004 V .. 1.0008 V at VREF = 1.04 V, LSB 1.962 mV)
cmp_x = 1  while isense_cmp > V_DAC(code_x)    i.e. shunt voltage > code_x × 0.1962 mV × (VREF / 1.04 V)
```

Two identical StrongARM comparators (`g1_cmp`) share `isense_cmp`; the soft one is strobed on the
rising edge of `cmp_clk`, the hard one on the falling edge (inverter inside `g1_trip`). Each has an
SR latch, so its output holds between strobes. Full scale (code 255) is 50.0 mV shunt voltage; the
pedestal (code 0 = 0.5 V) is the same fraction of VREF as the G1_SENSE pedestal, so the shunt-referred
threshold depends only on the code and on VREF, not on the OTA pedestal.

### Comparator `g1_cmp`

Razavi's modified StrongARM: clocked tail `sg13_lv_nmos` 16/0.13, input pair 12/0.34 (two devices),
NMOS and PMOS cross-coupled pairs 3/0.13, four PMOS precharge switches (P/Q nodes 3/0.13, X/Y 6/0.13),
two neutralisation dummies (6/0.13, gate on one input, source/drain on the opposite drain) for
differential kickback, output inverters skewed to a ≈ 0.35 V threshold (1/0.13 PMOS, 4/0.13 NMOS)
so the common-mode dip of X/Y during amplification (to ≈ 0.6–0.7 V) never reaches the NOR SR latch;
NOR SR latch 4/0.13 PMOS, 1.5/0.13 NMOS. No static current; no offset cancellation (the trip code
absorbs offset on the bench; see the accuracy budget in `INTERFACE.md`).

### DAC `g1_dac8`

530 series units of `rppd` 4 µm × 1.2 µm (≈ 95.4 Ω each, 50.6 kΩ total, 20.5 µA from the buffered
VREF of 1.04 V; the frozen netlist `sim/netlist/g1_dac8.spice`, generated by `schematic/gen_trip.py`).
Taps 255 … 510 (0.500 … 1.001 V) go into a binary tree of 510 `sg13_hv_nmos` 2/0.45 switches (8 levels,
2 per 2:1 mux) whose gates are driven at 3.3 V by eight level shifters (`g1_tlvlup`, structure of the
PDK IO `LevelUp` cell) and their complements. Thick-oxide switches were chosen after the first version
with 1.2 V transmission gates: their off-state leakage through 510 parallel paths redistributed the
string current and shifted every tap (8 LSB at 27 °C, 53 LSB at 175 °C with a 4.3 µA string). The
string current was also raised 5×. A 1 pF `cmim` hold capacitor sits on each DAC output in `g1_trip`.

## Source

Comparator core, inverters and SR latch start from `2AMLogic/sg13g2-comparator`
(`design/comparator.spice`, commit `d3484754a9ddc0874aa0f205e3f2259b1c7fdc13`, Apache-2.0; copy with
licence in `source/2AMLogic_sg13g2-comparator/`). Changes: tail current-bias branch removed (clocked
tail only), neutralisation dummies added, inverters re-skewed. The IHP AnalogAcademy module-3 dynamic
comparator was consulted for the PDK-specific netlisting conventions only; nothing was copied from it.
The DAC has no source (no SG13G2 string DAC exists in the open); its topology is the standard
resistor string with a tree decoder.

### Comparator: offset Monte Carlo (`sim/results_cmp_mc_cm0.75.txt`)

`mos_tt_mismatch`, 27 °C, 1.2 V, common mode 0.75 V, 10 MHz strobe, offset found with a 0.5 mV input
staircase (one step per strobe, resolution ±0.25 mV), six seeded runs (`rndseed` 1–6) of 16–20 samples,
n = 104: **σ = 4.55 mV, mean +0.9 mV, range −8.3 … +10.8 mV** at the comparator input, i.e.
**0.46 mV σ shunt-referred** (÷10: gain 20 and the ½ divider). The 2AMLogic source reports 8.4 mV
mean 3σ (2.8 mV σ) for its sizing with a biased tail; our clocked-tail version at a lower common mode
is about 1.6× worse, which the ±10 % trip budget (2.5 mV shunt-referred) absorbs without trim.

| 30.0 mV (1.1 mV below soft threshold) | 0.7994 / 0.8005 / 0.9987 V | ±34 mV / +0.001 mV | ±32 mV / +0.02 mV | ±43 mV / +0.06 mV | all 0 / all 0 (correct) |

Reading: each strobe kicks both comparator inputs by 30–40 mV peak (the common-mode tail kick
Razavi describes), and the 1 pF nodes recover within the period (mean shift ≤ 0.06 mV: the source
impedances of 20–25 kΩ against 1 pF give τ ≈ 25 ns ≪ 100 ns). The kick is common to both inputs of a
comparator, so what matters is the differential residue at the decision instant (≈ 0.5 ns after the
edge). It was not measured directly; the third row bounds it: the hard comparator decides "above"
with its quiet input 1.4 mV below the threshold, so the kick-induced decision error is at least
+1.4 mV at the comparator (**0.14 mV shunt-referred, 0.7 LSB**), of the same sign for every strobe
(systematic; absorbed by the bench-calibrated trip code). The soft comparator at 1.1 mV below its
threshold decided correctly. Both are far inside the ±10 % (2.5 mV shunt-referred) budget and below
the comparator's own mismatch offset (0.46 mV shunt-referred σ). Options if post-layout parasitics make
it worse: 2–4 pF hold capacitors (halves the peak), or the 5 MHz strobe.

### DAC string (matching)

The 530 units (`rppd` 4 µm × 1.2 µm, 95.4 Ω each) are identical PCell instances stacked at
1.76 µm pitch with **shared heads** (the PCell's contact bar and Metal1 head of unit *i* is the
head of unit *i*+1: identical shapes merge, the rule `Rppd.c` (SalBlock to contact 0.20 µm) holds
on both sides). The string is a serpentine of nine columns of 64 units at 4.36 µm pitch (pSD,
EXTBlock and SalBlock of neighbouring columns merge): four pedestal columns (255 units from
`vss` to tap 0, the first column holds 63), four tapped columns (units 255 … 510, one tap per unit
head, 64 taps each, chain direction alternating up/down) and a top column with the 19 units to
`vref`. Every column has one **dummy unit at each end** and a full **dummy column** closes the
array on both sides; the dummies are the physical unit (GatPoly, pSD, SalBlock, EXTBlock, contact
bars, Metal1 heads) without the `PolyRes` recognition layer, so they are not devices for the
extractor and their poly stubs hang off the chain ends with one end floating. Adjacent columns are
bridged head to head on Metal1 (pedestal) or Metal2 (across the switch columns). The taps leave
each head through a Via1 at its right end and a Metal2 jog to the switch pair beside it, so every
tap has the same head geometry and the same jog. Series units between the taps are combined by the
LVS (`combine_devices`) on both sides into the 255 tapped resistors, one pedestal resistor
(l = 306 µm) and one top resistor (l = 22.8 µm).

### Switch tree

The 510 `sg13_hv_nmos` 2/0.45 switches are placed as 255 **shared-source pairs** (two PCells
mirrored about their common source strip, rotated so that the 2 µm width runs along x: Activ
2.0 × 1.96 µm), each pair being one 2:1 mux of the schematic (drain *a* = even input, gate
`dhn<b>`; drain *b* = odd input, gate `dh<b>`). Beside each tapped string column: column A with
the 32 level-0 pairs at the tap pitch (3.52 µm per pair), and column BC with the 16 level-1 pairs
interleaved with the 15 pairs of levels 2 … 5 (their centres fall on distinct 3.52 µm slots);
levels 6 and 7 (three pairs) sit in a last column beyond the string. Gates are contacted on poly
tabs left (`dhn`) and right (`dh`) of each pair under **vertical Metal1 lanes** (one lane per
gate net per column, lanes at 0.5 µm pitch for the five levels of column BC); the 16 lanes of
every group drop through Via1/Via2 onto a 16-line **Metal3 gate bus** (0.5 µm pitch) below the
array. Inter-level connections are Metal2: horizontal jogs from the string heads to the level-0
drains, a vertical lane in the gap between columns A and BC for the level-0 → level-1 links, and
five vertical Metal2 lanes over column BC (0.46 µm pitch) for the links of levels 1 → 5; the four
level-5 outputs travel on Metal3 to the last column, the level-7 source is the DAC output. One
merged ThickGateOx covers each group of switch columns; a p+ substrate bar between every string
column and its column A (and one before the last column) keeps every switch within 9 µm of a
substrate tie (rule `LU.b`, 20 µm).

### Level shifters

The eight `g1_tlvlup` cells are a bank at the bottom of the DAC (one 7 µm column per bit): a row of
1.2 V inverters in their own n-wells (tied to `vdd`), a p+ tie bar, a row of four `sg13_hv_nmos`
1.9/0.45 (MN1, MN2, XO.MN, XOB.MN) and a row of four `sg13_hv_pmos` in one 3.3 V n-well with its n+
tie bar and the `vdda` rail on top. Each device column carries one net (`nb`, `n`, `y`, `yb`) on
a Metal2 vertical between the NMOS and PMOS drains; the gate cross-connections are Metal3 jogs; `y`
and `yb` continue on Metal2 (jogged to x positions that clear the lane drops) up to the gate bus.
Inputs `d0 … d7` are Metal2 pins at the DAC's bottom edge.

### Comparator

Two rows mirrored about the tail: NMOS row (tail 16/0.13 as four 4 µm fingers in the centre,
input pair 12/0.34 as two 6 µm fingers each, the neutralisation dummies 6/0.13 outside them, then
the cross-coupled 3/0.13, the inverter NMOS and the two latch NMOS per side), a channel of eight
Metal2 tracks (`qb xn xp clk xq yn q xnb|ynb` from bottom to top, 0.5 µm pitch) and the PMOS row
in one n-well (the precharge, cross-coupled, inverter and latch PMOS per side) with the `vdd` rail
and its n+ tie on top. Every terminal reaches its track through a Metal1 stub and a Via1; gates
are strapped on the side away from the channel (NMOS) or towards it (PMOS) and contacted beside
the Activ, with the left half a mirror image of the right half including the PCell orientation.
The tail node is a Metal2 bar between the source fingers of the pair; the tail gate is contacted
on both sides (x = ±1.47 µm) and `clk` reaches it on two Metal3 verticals so that it never crosses
the tail bar. The PMOS x positions are chosen so that every PMOS stub keeps 0.42 µm from every
NMOS stub of another net (identical vias of the same net merge).

*Parasitic symmetry.* The first version had the tracks in the order `xq xp xn yn clk xnb|ynb q qb`
with each stub ending at its own track and one tail contact; its extraction gave the two drain
nodes of the input pair 8.0 and 9.9 fF (the `xp` track had `xn` as a neighbour, `xq` had none, and
the `clk` track sat next to `yn` only), and the post-layout comparator decided wrongly with the
input 8 mV below the threshold (a > 8 mV systematic offset,
`sim/postlayout/results_postlayout_rev1.txt`, logs in `sim/postlayout/logs_rev1/`). The layout was
then symmetrised: `clk` lies between the two drain tracks, each drain track has its own latch node
(`xn`, `yn`) on the other side and each latch node an SR-latch output (static during the decision)
beyond it; **every Metal1 stub spans the whole channel** (6.35 … 10.35 µm, dead end beyond its own
track) so that the Metal1 of the two halves is identical and every stub crosses under every track
that spans its x, and the tail gate has two contacts. The cell-level extraction (kpex on `g1_cmp`
alone, same settings as the macro; `reports/pex/g1_cmp_cell/`) then gives `xp`/`xq` 12.43/12.40 fF,
`xn`/`yn` 18.12/18.09 fF, `clk` coupling 2.69/2.66 fF, the neighbour couplings `xp–xn` = `xq–yn`
= 3.78 fF: matched to 0.2 %, at the price of ≈ 2.5 fF more on each drain node (about 25 % more
wiring capacitance) and 2.8 fF more on `clk`. Pins: `inp`,
`inn`, `q`, `qb` on Metal2 at the left/right edges, `clk` (Metal3) and `vdd` (Metal2) at the top,
`vss` on the p+ ring. In the macro both comparators are mirrored in x, so `inn` (the DAC side)
faces the hold capacitor and `inp` (`icmp`) faces the other way: the comparator input wiring is a
single Metal3 line at y = 154.5 µm, 8 µm above the DAC tops and away from the code inputs, which
enter the DACs at their bottom edges 150 µm lower.

### Fill

The chip-level filler does not fill inside placed macros, so the macro carries its own fill:
`layout/fill_macro.sh` (with `layout/fill_macro.py`) runs the PDK filler
(`libs.tech/klayout/tech/scripts/filler.py`, fill macros `sg13g2_filler_{ActGatP,Metal}.lym`) on
the generated GDS. The PDK fill macros fill the *holes of the EdgeSeal layer*, so the script adds a
temporary EdgeSeal ring whose hole is the macro shrunk by 3 µm, fills, and removes the ring again;
TopMetal1/2 fill is not placed (`no_topmetal`: the chip PDN's TopMetal1/2 stripes cross the macro
and the chip filler fills TopMetal after routing). No-fill shapes (`<layer>/23`, written by the
generator and honoured by the PDK filler and the chip filler; 3 µm margin) keep the fill away from
the DAC bodies (strings and switch tree: Activ, GatPoly, Metal1–3), the comparators and the divider
cell (all layers), the hold capacitors (Metal4/5, TopMetal), and the `IOVDD` bar zone (Metal4/5,
TopMetal). Fill on the drawing-layer datatype 22 (`reports/fill/filler.log`): Activ 4.9 %, GatPoly
3.0 %, Metal1 5.3 %, Metal2 6.5 %, Metal3 4.4 %, Metal4 26.4 %, Metal5 30.9 % of the macro area
(drawn: Activ 7.7, GatPoly 14.7, Metal1 14.7, Metal2 3.7, Metal3 5.4, Metal4 1.4, Metal5 4.7,
TopMetal1 4.1 %) — the lower layers stay lightly filled because the DAC bodies (48 % of the area)
and the matched cells are excluded. The fill is floating metal on datatype 22: the LVS deck does not
read it (LVS unchanged, `reports/lvs/`), kpex's SG13G2 technology maps drawing and pin datatypes
only, so it is **not in the post-layout netlist** (`layout/pex_input.py` also strips it; the PEX
input of the filled macro is XOR-identical to the one the post-layout results were extracted from,
so those results stand), and the full DRC rule set is clean with it (`reports/drc/`).

the layout parasitics do not move it: the post-layout error is the same constant −0.004 LSB (−8 µV)

carries the comparator input pair and its neutralisation dummies, 12 fF of wiring, and the tree

stays well inside the 1 µs the digital macro waits after a code change (`INTERFACE.md`).

## Unverified

- Comparator noise (no transient-noise run) and metastability rate.
- Comparator offset MC at other common modes than 0.75 V and at temperature. The systematic
  post-layout offset was only bracketed by the two 1 mV decisions (between −2.0 and +0.2 mV at tt)
  and by the cell-level parasitic symmetry (0.2 %); it was not swept finely, and the residual
  asymmetry of the input wiring outside the cell (`icmp` on Metal3 from the divider, `vth` from the
  DAC output through the hold capacitor tab) was not separated from it. No post-layout Monte Carlo.
- DAC linearity through the switch tree with mismatch (only the string was Monte-Carlo'd; the
  switches carry no DC current so they add no static error beyond leakage, which was simulated at
  the corners); the post-layout 256-code sweep uses typical models without mismatch.
- Kickback after layout with the real G1_SENSE source (only the schematic deck `tb_kickback.cir`
  was run); the post-layout comparator run includes the extracted hold nodes and the DAC tree.
- Wiring resistance: kpex 0.3.12 RC mode is not usable (devices left off the resistor trees, see
  the G1_BGR README), so the post-layout netlist has capacitances only. Hand estimate from the PDK
  LEF values (Metal1 0.135 Ω/sq, Metal2–5 0.103 Ω/sq, contacts/vias 20–22 Ω per cut): a DAC tap
  path from the string head to the output crosses 16 vias (≈ 320 Ω) and ≈ 120 µm of 0.24 µm
  Metal2/Metal3 (≈ 50 Ω), i.e. ≈ 0.4 kΩ in series with the 7 thick-oxide switches (≈ 10 kΩ) and the
  string's 10–19 kΩ output impedance: < 2 % of the RC that sets the DAC settling and no effect on
  the DC transfer (the taps carry no DC current). The string itself is the extracted device value.
- The MIM capacitors were removed from the PEX input (kpex has no model for the MIM layer) and
  re-inserted as ideal `cap_cmim` devices: the parasitics of the plates to the wiring underneath
  are not in the post-layout netlist (the hold capacitors sit over the `vth`/`icmp`/`VREF` Metal3
  lines).
- The ThickGateOx layer was also removed from the PEX input (same tool limitation); the 3.3 V
  devices keep their models through the L = 0.45 µm rule of `make_pex_netlist.py`, but the extra
  capacitance of the thick gate oxide region is not extracted.
- `TRIP_SET` pad path (external hard-threshold override) is **not built**; it would replace the
  hard DAC output with the pad voltage through an analog switch.
- Density rules (chip level, after the chip filler). Note for the chip-level density check: the PDK
  density deck takes any `prBoundary` **189/0** shape in the GDS as the chip area (`Using prBoundary
  (189/0) for chip area` in the dry run's `klayout-density.log`, 54 340 µm² = the union of the
  `g1_sense`/`g1_gate` 189/0 boundaries), which is why the dry run reported the *maximum*-density
  rules `AFil.g1`, `M1.k` … `M5.k`, `TM1.d`, `TM2.d` over that column; this macro carries its
  boundary on 189/4 only.
