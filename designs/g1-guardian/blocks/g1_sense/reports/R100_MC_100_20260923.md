models remain `cap_typ`; capacitor mismatch is not qualified. Calibration is

All 100 retain the specified historical native-parameter controls. The changed
source therefore has its own electrical audit; historical population results
are not transferred wholesale.

## Narrow-margin checks

The worst residual has just 2 µV margin. Selected seeds 41039 (worst residual)
and 41053 (minimum gain) were separately rerun with `reltol=1e-5`, `vntol=1e-7`,
and `abstol=1e-14`, retaining the original 120-second bound, seed, source and
full temperature/grid protocol. Both passed numerical/native-parameter and
electrical checks. Printed ISENSE values did not change; this is not a claim
of equality below output precision or a general numerical-convergence proof.

source identities. The [compact evidence](r100-mc-100-evidence-20260923.json)

decks remain in the external results archive. Closed-loop OTA input differences
include finite gain and loading; algebraic terms are not isolated intrinsic
offsets or causal variance attribution. Population PSRR/CMRR/noise and full
integrated verification remain not run here; V07 remains open.
