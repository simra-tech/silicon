# Source-held actual-reference SENSE AC

This audit uses the gm4/comp3 SENSE, actual BGR586 and full TRIP circuit at
the qualified seed73001, TT25°C,3.3V operating point. Clock is held at its
original DC0 state. It is a schematic small-signal audit, not periodic
stability, physical PEX or a population result. Source models, mismatch
identities, loads and solver settings are unchanged.

All seven source/OP/AC leaves completed, with the nominal gain, bandwidth and
conditional main-loop phase/gain-margin screens passed. Broader stability
coverage remains not run; this is not a global stability acceptance.

The source-held baseline reproduces all11,512 realized parameters, legacy27,
all nine saved operating-point voltages and the first701 complex AC rows of
the completed loaded-noise audit exactly. The extended sweep has901 points,
1Hz–999999999.9999734Hz. Exact runtime/source/OP dispositions are retained
per leaf, not inferred from simulator exit status.

## Stimuli and measurement boundaries

The original one-sided stimulus changes the positive input alone. A separate
DC-zero ideal source supplies these testbench-only AC bases:

- Differential: positive+0.5V,negative−0.5V, differential1V and common0V.
- Common mode: positive1V,negative1V, differential0V and common1V.
- Rail response: AC1V on only VDDA or only VDD, other independent AC drives0.

DC inputs remain positive25mV and negative0V. Differential/common input
phasors are exported and checked explicitly. The exact rational identity
`one-sided = differential + common/2` is independently tested. Its numerical
response residual is reported separately; it is not claimed bit-exact.

CMRR and input-referred PSRR are `20 log10(|Ad|/|Ac or Asupply|)`. The separate
`20 log10(|Asupply|)` output-ripple transfer is also reported, retaining the
historical output-transfer convention without conflating the two. Neither
CMRR nor PSRR has an allocated acceptance limit.

## Conditional feedback method

The existing two-injection Tian fixture is placed at the main OTA negative
input. Voltage source polarity is e−f, current injection is ground→e and
measured feedback current is negative current through the voltage probe.
The source transformation has an exact inverse and does not split or rename
any canonical compact device or mismatch identity. All other internal,
pedestal, reference and BGR loops remain closed. There is no proof this wire
breaks every return path, so the result cannot establish global stability.

The implemented return ratio is the repository's existing expression from
[Tian et al.,2001](https://kenkundert.com/docs/cd2001-01.pdf). Independent
one-pole complex-transfer/unity controls pass. Phase is unwrapped; unity
downcrossings and all odd180° crossings in either direction are recorded.
Interpolation is logarithmic in frequency. No crossing in the finite band
is not reported as infinite gain margin. Screening limits remain phase
margin≥60°,gain margin≥10dB; closed-loop gain19.9–20.1 and bandwidth≥2MHz.

Probe DC equivalence uses the established1µV voltage/1nA supply-current gates.
Exact equality is independently reported and is not implied by bounded
equivalence. The new Tian node inherits the unchanged1Tohm global shunt;
its signed DC current is explicitly recorded. The voltage/current injection
leaves must have exactly equal DC observations and source-probe bytes.

Both actual injection leaves meet those exact inter-leaf checks. Relative to
the unprobed baseline, their largest voltage difference is14.2719nV and
supply-current difference0.43682pA: bounded equivalence passes, exact equality
fails and remains recorded. Added-node shunt current is71.6521fA. Additional
negative-real crossings at146.3744MHz and424.9359MHz have gain margins91.3496dB
and135.8199dB; all sampled odd180° crossings, not merely the first, are included.

## Preserved failures and exclusions

Baseline r1 completed simulation but failed the missing-internal-vector
audit: inherited saves omitted xs.vp. Recovery adds only output saves before
the same OP command. Initial host control preparation failed on an unsupported
Python3.6 subprocess keyword before simulation; equivalent host syntax fixed
it. A host analytic exp/log control initially demanded impossible bit-exact
roundoff; its explicit1e-14 analytic bound does not alter circuit acceptance.
Original failed evidence remains separate from completed runs.

A fresh coordinated resource gate deferred the two loop runs at budget44
against reserved45; no child was launched. A later fresh47 gate permitted
the exact remaining leaves. Completed leaves were not rerun.

All model warnings remain recorded. The existing native intrinsic/field
reference-plane and complete-extraction failures remain unresolved. No
geometry, model-card, rule-deck or electrical-population change is made.
Each successful leaf retains resistor-vmax warnings and one model
temperature-limiter NaN warning during OP; finite final vectors do not waive
model-scope applicability.
