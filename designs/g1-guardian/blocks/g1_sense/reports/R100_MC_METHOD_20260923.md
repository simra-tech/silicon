# R100 standalone Monte Carlo method control

**Passed method control, not population or physical qualification.** The
selected historical worst seed 41039 was replayed on the original gm4/comp3
source and on C45/R100. Both completed under the original 120-second bound:
42.682 and 41.190 seconds respectively. These timings are not a speed benchmark.

The historical observation-only replay reproduced all 36 printed OP rows and
51 legacy parameters exactly. Each source preserved all 529 queried native
parameters across 25, −40, 125 and return-to-25 °C; the return rows were exact.
Across sources, 528 queries were identical and the declared main CMIM query
satisfied its native area relation. The original `cap_typ` library was held:
this is **not qualification of capacitor mismatch**. Historical warning
family/instance counts also matched. Six parser controls and three subsequent
missing-group/missing-query/reordering negative controls passed.

Both sources had simulated gain 19.9346–19.9356 V/V and worst frozen,
continuous-correction residual 498 µV, against the strict <500 µV requirement.
The margin is only 2 µV. Calibration remains ideal and continuous at room
temperature, SENSE_N=0, shunt=25 mV; it is not digital trim reach or joint-chain
yield. Of 27 non-return observations, 21 have true average common mode in the
supported interval; the six outside-range diagnostics remain retained.
