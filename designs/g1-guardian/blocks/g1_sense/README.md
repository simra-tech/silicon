## What it is

Difference amplifier with a single two-stage OTA and four `rppd` resistor arms
(R1 = 10 kΩ, R2 = 20 × R1 = 200 kΩ, all 10 kΩ units of 2 µm × 76.7 µm):

```
ISENSE   = VPED + 20 × (SENSE_P − SENSE_N)      VPED = (51/53) × VREF = 1.0008 V at VREF = 1.04 V
vref_buf = VREF                                 (OTA follower; feeds the two G1_TRIP DAC strings)
```

- Inputs `SENSE_P`/`SENSE_N` (analog pads): 0–50 mV differential, common mode −0.1 to +0.3 V.
  The OTA inputs then sit at −0.05 to +0.38 V, which the thick-oxide PMOS pair accepts.
- Output `ISENSE` 1.0–2.0 V in the 3.3 V domain; G1_TRIP halves it to 0.5–1.0 V for the 1.2 V comparators.
- The pedestal is a 2:51 `rppd` divider (53 units, 2 µA) from the buffered VREF, buffered again by a
  second instance of the same OTA (`XBUF`, unity gain). A pedestal error reaches the shunt-referred trip
  point divided by 20. 51/53 × 1.04 V = 1.0008 V; the DAC bottom tap in G1_TRIP is the same fraction
  (255/530 × 2) so the shunt-referred threshold depends on the code and VREF only.
- `XREF`, a third OTA instance, buffers the bandgap's `VREF` (its 88 kΩ source impedance cannot drive
  the 2 × 50 kΩ DAC strings); output `vref_buf`. Its offset (σ 3.8 mV, same cell as the MC below) is a
  0.4 % scale error of every threshold.
- Bias: `iptat` (4.13 µA at 27 °C from G1_BGR, PTAT) into one `sg13_hv_nmos` 3.3 µm/1 µm diode `MBI`;
  its gate voltage `vbn` biases the three OTAs at the density of the original 10 µA/8 µm design point.
  All OTA currents therefore scale with absolute temperature (0.77× at −40 °C, 1.49× at 175 °C).
- Supply current, three OTAs: 0.73 mA (−40 °C), 0.98 mA (27 °C), 1.54 mA (175 °C) — 3.2 mW at 27 °C
  and 3.3 V, 5.1 mW at 175 °C (simulated). Revision A with two OTAs at fixed bias was 0.68 mA.

Schematics: `schematic/g1_ota.sch` (OTA), `schematic/g1_sense.sch` (top), symbols alongside; both
are generated by `schematic/gen_sense.py` (device sizes and topology live there) and netlisted by
`schematic/netlist.sh` into `sim/netlist/*.spice`, which the decks include.

### OTA (`g1_ota`)

Two-stage: PMOS-input folded cascode (input pair `sg13_hv_pmos` 2 × 96 µm / 2 µm, 80 µA tail,
60 µA PMOS sources, 100 µA NMOS sources, wide-swing (Sooch) cascode bias from the `vbn` rail)
followed by a PMOS common-source stage with a 120 µA NMOS load, Miller compensated (`cmim` 0.8 pF,
`rppd` 1.7 kΩ nulling resistor). Open-loop gain of the first stage alone 890 (59 dB, simulated);
the second stage was needed because the resistor network loads the output with ≈ 67 kΩ, which a
single-stage OTA (output resistance ≈ 3 MΩ) cannot drive: the closed-loop gain measured 9.5 instead
of 20 with the single stage. A PMOS (not NMOS) second stage was chosen so that the first-stage output
balances near the mirror node voltage; this reduced the systematic offset from 2.2 mV to 0.07 mV.

https://github.com/IHP-GmbH/TO_Nov2024 commit `a0f364b8e40458e480f079547ea8846b1db8da41`,

The high-frequency PSRR is the weak point of the PMOS second stage: at 1 MHz a 100 mV ripple on
`IOVDD` gives 45 mV on ISENSE, i.e. 2.2 mV shunt-referred. `IOVDD` is shared with the pad ring,
so the 30 mA `GATE` driver's supply transient will reach ISENSE; the comparators' hold capacitors
and the digital blanking (N samples) are what reject it. Documented as a known limitation; a
separate analog supply pad is not available in the 24-pin map.

### Monte Carlo (mismatch)

Run on **revision A** (VREF 1.2 V, pedestal 5/6, two OTAs at fixed 10 µA bias): 200 samples (two seeded
runs of 100, `rndseed` 1 and 2), `mos_tt_mismatch` for LV and HV devices plus `res_typ_mismatch`, 27 °C,
3.3 V; each sample evaluated at input common mode 0 V and 0.3 V. It was not repeated on revision B
(deck `tb_sense_mc.cir` is updated for it): the offset is the input pair's threshold mismatch, which the
pedestal ratio, the reference value and the bias source do not change; what revision B adds is the
`vref_buf` offset (same cell, σ 3.8 mV) as a 0.4 % scale error, not an offset. Two samples lost a value to an ngspice echo glitch (n = 198). Summary in
`sim/results_sense_mc_27C.txt`:

| Quantity (shunt-referred) | mean | sigma | min / max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total offset, (ISENSE(0) − 1.0 V)/20, CM = 0 | +0.07 mV | **4.00 mV** | −9.4 / +10.5 mV |
| Main OTA offset alone (vp − vn) | −0.08 mV | 3.81 mV | −10.1 / +9.1 mV |
| Pedestal VPED (buffer offset + divider) | 0.9999 V | 3.7 mV (→ 0.19 mV shunt-referred) | 0.9905 / 1.008 V |
| Total offset at CM = 0.3 V | +0.04 mV | 3.91 mV | |
| Change of offset for a 0.3 V common-mode step (resistor mismatch) | +3 µV | 41 µV | |

The offset is the thick-oxide input pair's threshold mismatch (2 × 96 µm × 2 µm already); the
resistor network contributes 0.04 mV per 0.3 V of common mode, the pedestal path 0.19 mV. Untrimmed,
3σ = 12 mV shunt-referred against a 0.5 mV target: the offset **must** be trimmed, and the trim must
cover the OTA, not the resistors.

## Trim

Decision: **no analog trim network; the trim is a signed code offset applied by the digital macro to
both threshold DACs** (`sense_ofs`, see `../g1_trip/INTERFACE.md`). Reason: the threshold DAC already
resolves 0.196 mV shunt-referred per LSB (`../g1_trip/README.md`), which is finer than the 0.5 mV
target, and the trip code is measured on the bench anyway; an analog trim in the 3.3 V domain would
need its own current DAC and mux, add its own mismatch, and could not be finer.

Sizing from the MC: σ = 4.0 mV → cover ±6σ = ±24 mV → **8-bit signed offset, LSB 0.196 mV
(one DAC LSB), range ±127 LSB = ±24.9 mV**; residual after trim ≤ ½ LSB = 0.1 mV plus the
temperature drift of the offset, which was **not** simulated (MC ran at 27 °C only). The trim is
applied identically to soft and hard thresholds and reduces the usable hard-threshold range by up
to 127 codes in the worst case (at ±3σ by 61 codes, i.e. the hard threshold still reaches 38 mV = 1.5×
of a 25 mV nominal). What the trim does not remove: the CM-dependent term (41 µV per 0.3 V, negligible)
and gain error (0.06 %, negligible).

### OTA cell `g1_ota` (97.66 × 51.2 µm, all pins on its east edge)

Four device rows, south to north, all fingers 8 µm (the HV PCells refuse fingers above 10 µm,
so the schematic's 12/16 µm fingers are re-cut; total W and L are what LVS compares):

| row | devices (west → east) | fingers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N | MB4 · MB2 · MB6 · M3 · M13 · M16 · M4 · M21 | `nmosHV`, L 1 µm; M3/M13 and M16/M4 mirror-symmetric about the row centre |
| P-A | M14 · M15 · M12 · M11 | `pmosHV`, L 1 µm, 12 × 8 µm each; sources outside, cascodes inside |
| P-B | M1/M2 input pair | `pmosHV` L 2 µm, 32 fingers × 6 µm interdigitated **ABBAABBAABBAABBA** (finger pairs share a drain strip; 1-D common centroid), poly dummies at both ends |
| P-C | MB7 · MB5 · MB3 · MT · M20 | `pmosHV`, L 1 µm; MT 16 × 8, M20 24 × 8 |

Every row has poly-only dummy fingers in the 1.4 µm gaps between devices and at both ends (no
Activ, so they are not devices and the frozen netlist stays LVS-exact). Metal use: Metal1
source/drain rails and gate pads at the devices, Metal2 gate rails and horizontal exit wires,
Metal3 verticals in the two side channels (west: fn mir vbp vbpc vbnc, east: fp out1 out) plus the
tail bus, Metal3 vdd/vss trunks (1.2 µm) on both sides, Metal4 vdd/vss bars over the source rails
(via stacks every 2–4 fingers). Bulks: the three PMOS rows sit in one NWell with an n+ ring and
two n+ strips between the rows (LU.a); the whole cell has a p+ substrate ring (LU.b). Miller
capacitor `cmim` 23 × 23 µm (Metal5 bottom plate = `out`, TopMetal1 top plate = `cz`) over rows
P-A/P-B; the nulling resistor RZ (`rppd` 1 × 6.2 µm) sits in the east channel over the substrate.
Pins: `inn`, `inp`, `vbn`, `out` Metal2 stubs on the east edge; `vdd`/`vss` Metal3 trunks to the
north/south edges and Metal4 bars to the east/west edges.

### Macro `g1_sense`

| region | content |
| --- | --- |
| west | the three OTA cells stacked (XOTA south, XBUF, XREF north, 4 µm apart); their vdd/vss Metal3 trunks are joined across the gaps and end on the north vdd bar / south vss bar |
| middle | routing channel: one Metal3 vertical per net (vbn/iptat, vn, isense, vp, two R2 link nets, vped, vped_ref, vref_buf, vref, vss) at 1 µm pitch; the bias diode MBI (`nmosHV` 3.3/1) at its south end |
| east | the resistor array: **95 units of `rppd` 2 × 76.7 µm, one PCell per schematic unit**, 2.5 µm column pitch, two meander rows of 52 columns with poly dummy columns at both ends, one merged pSD/SalBlock/EXTBlock cover per row; row 0 (south) `[d d][RD2a 13][R2N a 10][R1N][R1P][R2P a 10][RD2b 13][d d]`, row 1 `[d d][RD2c 13][R2P b 10][RD1 2][R2N b 10][RD2d 12][d][d d]`; own p+ ring |

R2N and R2P each have one 10-unit half west and one east of the centre, one in each row
(2-D common centroid at chain level, so a linear gradient cancels in the gain ratio); R1N and
R1P are the adjacent centre columns of row 0, and their `SENSE_N`/`SENSE_P` heads leave on the same
side with the same track length ±2.5 µm. Chain ends are unit heads; Metal3 drops take them to
Metal2 tracks in the three gaps (south of row 0, between the rows, north of row 1), which run to
the channel or to the east edge. No metal crosses a resistor body. The two resistor-network nodes
that carry the amplifier's input are `vn` (R1N–R2N) and `vp` (R1P–R2P); their tracks and channel
verticals are the same length within 3 µm.

Why one PCell per unit: the PDK LVS deck's resistor series combiner (`custom_combiner.lvs`,
`supp_series`) merges two resistors that share a node without checking whether that node has other
connections. With split half-units it merged R1N+R2N *through* `vn` on both the layout and the
schematic side, leaving the OTA input pin dangling on both, and reported PASS; that comparison
would not have seen a missing `vn` wire. With 95 full-length units and `--no_series_res` the
compare is 1:1 (each layout resistor against its schematic unit) and complete.

### Fill and no-fill (integration file: `layout/g1_sense_filled.gds`)

The chip filler does not fill inside placed macros, so the macro carries its own fill
(`../g1_padring/INTEGRATION.md`, density finding). No-fill regions (the PDK `NoFillerStack` PCell:
Activ, GatPoly, Metal1–5, TopMetal1/2 on datatype 23, plus `NoMetFiller` 160/0) cover, with a 3 µm
margin, the input pair row and the Miller capacitor of every OTA (inside the `g1_ota` cell) and the
whole resistor array; the chip filler honours the same layers. `layout/fill.sh` (log
`layout/fill_g1_sense.log`) runs the PDK filler `libs.tech/klayout/tech/scripts/filler.py` (all
three macros: Activ/GatPoly, Metal1–5, TopMetal1/2, default distances) on the macro: because those
macros fill `EdgeSeal.holes` only, `layout/fill_prep.py` first draws a temporary EdgeSeal ring 1 µm
inside the boundary and deletes it again from the result, so the fill stays 1 µm inside the macro
edge and no seal-ring layer remains. Fill instances in `g1_sense_filled.gds`: 146 Activ/GatPoly,
727 Metal1, 860 Metal2, 504 Metal3, 730 Metal4, 656 Metal5, 65 TopMetal1, 69 TopMetal2 cells
(datatype 22). Layer densities inside the boundary after fill (drawn + fill): Activ 18.5 %, GatPoly
16.8 %, Metal1 9.1 %, Metal2 10.8 %, Metal3 11.4 %, Metal4 10.0 %, Metal5 15.6 %, TopMetal1 10.5 %,
TopMetal2 7.2 % — low because the no-fill regions cover about 70 % of the macro; the 35 % global
metal minimum (`Mn.j`) is a chip-level window rule, not a macro rule. The boundary is on 189/4
(`prBoundary.boundary`) **only**: the chip-level density deck takes 189/0 as the chip outline, so no
macro GDS (including `g1_ota.gds`) carries a 189/0 shape. `g1_sense.gds` (unfilled generator output)
and `g1_sense.lef` are unchanged by the fill (same boundary, pins and OBS). SHA-256 of the delivered
GDS files (both blocks): `layout/gds_sha256.txt`.

## Post-layout (kpex 2.5D, capacitances)

PEX: `kpex 0.3.12` (KLayout-PEX), engine `--2.5D --mode CC`, with `layout/g1_sense.cdl` as schematic
(`reports/pex/cc/kpex_plain.log`, netlist `reports/pex/cc/g1_sense_k25d_pex_netlist.spice`: 942
capacitors, 2.6 pF in total; per net ≈ 76 fF on `vn`, 84 fF on `vp`, 128 fF on `isense`, 42–44 fF on
each folded node). Three things about this version of kpex had to be worked around, all recorded in
the docstring of `sim/postlayout/make_pex_netlist.py`:

1. its technology description has no capacitance model for the MIM layer (`"<TODO>"`), and the 2.5D
   engine aborts on it (`reports/pex/kpex_with_mim_failed.log`) — the extraction ran on a copy of the
   GDS without the MIM (36/0) and Vmim (129/0) layers and the three Miller capacitors are added back
   to the netlist as the schematic devices (`cap_cmim` 23 × 23 µm); the Metal5/TopMetal1 plates stay
   and contribute their fringe capacitance as plain metal;
2. it extracts the parasitics of the top cell's own shapes only (with the `g1_ota` cells in place it
   wrote no capacitor on any OTA-internal net, `reports/pex/cc_hier/`) — so it ran on the flattened GDS;
3. after a plain flatten the three copies of the OTA label `inp` collided and the net kept as `inp`
   received no capacitor at all (`reports/pex/cc_flat_labels/`, the asymmetric result that gave a
   −20 dB common-mode gain at 1 MHz) — the child cell's labels are stripped before flattening.

`sim/postlayout/make_pex_netlist.py` turns the kpex output into `sim/postlayout/g1_sense_pex.spice`
(`M$`/`R$` → PDK subcircuit calls with the per-finger junction areas kpex measured, `Cext` →
capacitors, port order of the schematic, the internal nodes the testbench nodesets name identified
by connectivity and called `xota_tail`, `xbuf_out1`, …). `sim/postlayout/run_postlayout.sh` runs
`tb_sense.cir` on it (same loads and options; the load resistors' floating `sub!` node is tied to
ground in the post-layout deck) at tt 27 °C, tt 175 °C, tt −40 °C, ss/wcs 175 °C and ff/bcs 27 °C;
`sim/postlayout/compare.py` writes `sim/postlayout/results/compare.md` (all 13 quantities, all five
