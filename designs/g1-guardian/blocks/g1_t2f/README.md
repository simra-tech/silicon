## Revision 2 (2026-09-19) — why revision 1 is superseded

Revision 1 (schematic frozen 2026-09-18, laid out and PEX'd 2026-09-19) ran the four comparator HBTs
`QA1 QB1 QA2 QB2` outside the `npn13G2` model card's safe operating area: their emitters sit at
V<sub>th</sub> − V<sub>BE</sub> ≈ 0.3 V while the collectors sat on the PMOS mirror load, i.e. at
V<sub>DD</sub> − |V<sub>GS</sub>| ≈ 2.5 V on the diode side and at V<sub>DD</sub> on the off side —
V<sub>CE</sub> = 2.29 V / 3.08 V at 3.6 V and 175 °C against `vce_max = 1.6` in
`libs.tech/ngspice/models/sg13g2_hbt_mod.lib` (the VBIC card has avalanche parameters `avc1/avc2`, so the
model produces the avalanche current). The avalanche base current of the conducting HBT flowed out of
its base into the threshold node and raised V<sub>th</sub> with V<sub>DD</sub> (+1 / +8 / +23 mV at
3.0 / 3.3 / 3.6 V in PTAT mode), which was the −9.3 °C/V supply sensitivity recorded for revision 1.
Revision 2 fixes it in the schematic with HV NMOS collector cascodes on every comparator HBT (below);
nothing else of the topology changed. Revision 1's sources and evidence are kept unchanged under
`rev1/` (`rev1/xschem/`, `rev1/layout/`, `rev1/reports/`, `rev1/sim/results/`,
`rev1/sim/postlayout/`, and the revision-1 README as `rev1/README_rev1_snapshot.md`); the same V<sub>CE</sub>
decks were re-run on the revision-1 netlist for the before/after table (`sim/logs/vce_rev1_*.log`).
Revision 2 also splits R<sub>REF</sub> in the schematic into the ten 24.5 µm units of the layout (LVS
one-to-one, see "Extractor view"); that adds the units' heads to the simulated REF-mode resistance
(about +1 %) and changes nothing in PTAT mode.

## What it is

A relaxation oscillator whose frequency is proportional to absolute temperature. It
reuses the bandgap's ΔV<sub>BE</sub> core: `G1_BGR` biases its HBT pair (1 unit against
8 parallel units, `npn13G2`) and converts ΔV<sub>BE</sub> across R1 (`rppd`) into
I<sub>PTAT</sub>; this block copies that current on the shared mirror rails (`pbias`,
`pcasc`) and charges two `cmim` capacitors alternately against a threshold derived from
V<sub>REF</sub>:

f = I<sub>PTAT</sub> / (2 C V<sub>th</sub>) = (kT/q) ln 8 / (2 R1 C V<sub>REF</sub>)

Schematic `xschem/g1_t2f.sch`, netlist `xschem/g1_t2f.spice` (generated by
`xschem/gen_g1_t2f.py`, netlisted with `xschem -n -q -x`):

- Four PMOS legs on the bandgap rails (same W/L 10/4 µm devices as the bandgap mirror, the first
  three cascoded on `pcasc` with the cascode bodies tied to source): I<sub>PTAT</sub> to the capacitor
  steering node, 2·I<sub>PTAT</sub> into R<sub>REF</sub> (ten `rppd` 0.5 × 24.5 µm units in
  series, 126 kΩ plus their heads, one unit per layout PCell), I<sub>PTAT</sub> into an NMOS
  diode that biases the comparator tails, and (revision 2) I<sub>PTAT</sub> into the cascode-bias
  follower below; that fourth leg (`MPF`) has no `pcasc` cascode because at 3.0 V and 175 °C in REF
  mode its drain sits at 2.45 V and there is only 0.55 V of headroom, and its current only sets a
  gate voltage.
- Two capacitors C1, C2 (`cap_cmim` 28 × 28 µm, 1.18 pF each). PMOS switches steer
  I<sub>PTAT</sub> into one while an NMOS switch holds the other discharged, so the
  reset time does not enter the period.
- Two comparators, one per capacitor: `npn13G2` differential pair on an 8 µA PTAT tail
  (constant g<sub>m</sub>, so the delay is nearly temperature independent), **HV NMOS collector
  cascodes `MCA`/`MCB` (4/1 µm) between the HBTs and the PMOS mirror load (revision 2)**, two HV
  inverters. The cascode gate rail `vcg` = `vth` + |V<sub>GS</sub>| is made by a PMOS source
  follower `MSF` (2/2 µm, body tied to source) whose gate sits on the threshold node and whose
  current is the fourth I<sub>PTAT</sub> leg; every HBT collector therefore rests at
  `vth` + (|V<sub>GS,MSF</sub>| − V<sub>GS,MC</sub>) ≈ `vth` + 0.1 V, i.e. V<sub>CE</sub> ≈ V<sub>BE</sub> + 0.1 V
  on the conducting side in both modes and at every temperature, and the follower loads `vth`
  only with a gate. NMOS bleeders `MBA`/`MBB` (0.5/2 µm, gates on `nbias`, about 0.25 µA) keep the
  cascode of the non-conducting HBT in conduction, so that collector cannot float above
  `vcg` − V<sub>GS</sub>(0.25 µA); the bleed is equal on both sides of the mirror load and does not
  move the switching point. A NAND RS latch (`q`, `qn`) flips at each crossing; `en` = 0
  resets it (3-input NAND) and discharges both capacitors.
- Threshold multiplexer `mode`: 0 → V<sub>th</sub> = V<sub>REF</sub> (PTAT reading);
  1 → V<sub>th</sub> = 2 I<sub>PTAT</sub> R<sub>REF</sub> (reference reading,
  f = 1/(4 R<sub>REF</sub> C), temperature independent to first order).
- Output: `q` is shifted to the 1.2 V domain with two HV NMOS pull-downs and a
  cross-coupled LV PMOS pair (no static current) and buffered by two LV inverters to
  `fout`, which drives the `TEMP_OUT` pad (`sg13g2_IOPadOut16mA`). No divider is
  needed: 1.2 to 2.4 MHz over −40 .. 175 °C.

Pins: `vdd (3.3 V) vdd12 (1.2 V) vss pbias pcasc vref en mode fout`, unchanged from revision 1.
Control bits and their register mapping: `INTERFACE.md` (unchanged).

### Why the cascodes are NMOS

A common-gate device clamps its *source*; between an HBT collector (which sinks current) and a
load from V<sub>DD</sub> (which sources it) the clamped terminal must face the HBT, so the cascode is
an NMOS with its source on the collector, exactly as the bandgap's `MNC1/MNC2`. A PMOS in that
position would clamp the load side and leave the collector free. The `pcasc` rail itself is
unsuitable as the gate bias because the collectors must follow `vth` (PTAT in REF mode: 0.79 V at
−40 °C, 1.58 V at 175 °C); the follower on `vth` gives that tracking with no DC load.

### Ratio readout

The comparator delay t<sub>d</sub> adds to each half period: T = 2 (C V<sub>th</sub>/I + t<sub>d</sub>).
At 27 °C the simulated overshoot of the capacitor above the threshold is about 38 mV,
i.e. t<sub>d</sub> ≈ 11 ns on a 310 ns half period (3.5 %). Because the tail current is
PTAT, t<sub>d</sub> is nearly constant and the two-point fit absorbs most of it; the
residual is in the tables below. The REF mode uses the same current, the same
capacitors and the same comparators with only the threshold changed, so
f<sub>PTAT</sub>/f<sub>REF</sub> = 2 ΔV<sub>BE</sub> R<sub>REF</sub> / (R1 V<sub>REF</sub>)
cancels C, the absolute threshold and, to first order, t<sub>d</sub>. Only the `rppd`
ratio R<sub>REF</sub>/R1 and the bandgap quantities remain. R<sub>REF</sub> is 0.5 µm wide and the
bandgap's R1 1 µm wide (same type and, in layout, the same orientation), so the ratio carries the
width-dependent part of the `rppd` sheet resistance as a fixed factor; the two-point calibration of
the ratio removes it. This is the twin-oscillator / voltage-ratio idea in time-multiplexed form; the
digital side only has to count both modes with the same gate. **In revision 2 the ratio readout is
the less linear of the two** (see the f(T) table): revision 1's REF-mode threshold was pulled up by
the avalanche current in a way that happened to flatten f<sub>REF</sub>; without it f<sub>REF</sub> shows
the real temperature coefficient of 1/(4 R<sub>REF</sub> C) plus the comparator delay, and the
two-point ratio residual reaches +4.9 °C at the ends of the range. The plain PTAT reading is the
calibrated one.

## Source

None on SG13G2 for this block. The bandgap it hangs on is `../g1_bgr` (see its
"Source" for the 2AMLogic lineage). The two-capacitor alternating relaxation scheme and
the reference-oscillator ratio follow published T2F sensors (Xu et al., Sensors 2023;
the ratio principle of Souri, Chae, Makinwa, JSSC 2013); no text or figure is copied. The
NMOS collector cascode is the remedy the bandgap applied to its own HBTs (`MNC1/MNC2`,
`../g1_bgr/README.md`, "What it is"); the follower-derived gate rail is this block's own, because its
collectors have to follow the threshold node.

### Fill (chip-level density finding)

The chip-level filler does not fill inside placed macros (`../g1_padring/INTEGRATION.md`, density
row), so the block carries its own fill. `g1_t2f_layout.py` draws the no-fill shapes that the PDK
`NoFillerStack` PCell would write — `<layer>/23` for Activ, GatPoly, Metal1 .. Metal5, TopMetal1,
TopMetal2 and `NoMetFiller` 160/0 — over the comparator HBT row including the dummies
(3.85 .. 45.65 × 64.67 .. 75.78 µm), the R<sub>REF</sub> serpentine (68.0 .. 85.1 × 1.4 .. 30.93 µm) and
the two `cmim` capacitors with their tabs and strap (2.4 .. 65.6 × 1.6 .. 37.4 µm), each with a 2 µm
margin, plus two rules of its own: Metal4, Metal5, TopMetal1 and TopMetal2 are no-fill over the
whole macro, because the LEF obstructs those layers only over the capacitor plates and the chip

and bar margins outside the Metal3 obstruction (x < 3.4, x > 89.9, y < 3.0, y > 99.6 µm). The chip

## Post-layout (kpex 2.5D, capacitances)

PEX: `kpex 0.3.12`, engine `--2.5D --mode CC --blackbox true`, on `layout/g1_t2f.gds` with the
LVS CDL as schematic (`reports/pex/cc/kpex_plain.log`, `kpex_stdout.log`; the GDS of record includes the block fill, which the extraction ignores, see "Fill"):

```
G1_WORKDIR=build/g1_t2f_rev2 flow/run.sh kpex --pdk ihp_sg13g2 --gds .../layout/g1_t2f.gds --cell g1_t2f \
    --schematic .../layout/g1_t2f_lvs.cdl --2.5D --mode CC --blackbox true --out_dir ...
```

Two limitations of the tool, both visible in the logs and both already met in revision 1: (1) the
kpex technology file maps the MIM plate layer to an unnamed layer (`cmim_top` → `<TODO>`), and
without `--blackbox true` the 2.5D engine stops with `KeyError: '<TODO>'`; with it the `cap_cmim`
device geometry is excluded from the field extraction, so the netlist carries the wiring and tab
parasitics of `cap1`/`cap2` but no parasitic of the MIM plates themselves, and (2) kpex's SPICE writer
stops at the first `cap_cmim` device (`Invalid parameter name: 'C'`) after all MOS, HBT and resistor
devices have been written. (kpex's own LVS pass, run with `top_lvl_pins=true`, reports "netlists don't
match" because every labelled internal net becomes a top-level pin; the PDK LVS run of record passes,
and the extraction does not depend on that compare — the device view and the capacitance CSV are
byte-identical with the single-resistor and the ten-unit CDL.) `sim/postlayout/make_pex_netlist.py`
therefore takes the 91 devices from the partial `reports/pex/cc/g1_t2f_k25d_pex_netlist.spice`
(75 MOS with their fingers unmerged, 6 HBT entries, the ten R<sub>REF</sub> units), the 398

## Unverified

- 77 K: the −196 °C run extrapolates the PDK models. Published measurements on an
  SG13G2 HBT show the ideality factor rising to about 1.35 .. 1.4 at 77 K (T<sub>eff</sub>
  ≈ 105 K) and the I–V family collapsing below ~60 K, so a linear PTAT reading at 77 K
  will read high by roughly +28 K and the sensitivity vanishes below ~60 K. The
  calibrated range is −40 .. 150 °C (QFN limit); 77 K is a characterisation point for
  n(T), not a temperature reading. No accuracy is claimed there.
- Package stress shift of V<sub>BE</sub> and ΔV<sub>BE</sub> after QFN assembly:
  calibrate packaged parts, not wafers.
- Comparator offset and its drift (HBT pair, no MC run), base-current loading of the
  threshold node (about 27 nA into 88 kΩ at V<sub>REF</sub>, 126 kΩ at V<sub>R</sub>;
  β-dependent, hence not PTAT; this is what the follower gate does *not* add to), charge injection
  of the steering switches, and the `cmim` temperature coefficient are all inside the simulated
  numbers but not separately bounded.
- The remaining supply sensitivity (-2.5 °C/V schematic, -3.3 °C/V post-layout) is attributed to the
  comparator delay's dependence on the V<sub>DD</sub>-referenced load swing; not isolated by a
  dedicated run. A comparator output stage whose switching point sits |V<sub>GS</sub>| below
  V<sub>DD</sub> (a PMOS common-source stage with a current-sink load, sketched but never simulated
  in an earlier session and not in the tree) would address it; not done.
- REF mode at −40 °C runs with a starved tail (V<sub>th</sub> = 0.79 V, tail node at 20 mV); the mode
  still oscillates at all nine temperatures, but its −40 °C point and the ratio readout's residual at
  the ends of the range (+4.9 °C) are what they are. The PTAT reading is the calibrated one.
- The V<sub>CE</sub> margin is simulated with the PDK model card; the reliability behaviour of `npn13G2`
  between the ≈ 1.1 V now reached and the 1.6 V limit is the foundry's number, not ours.
- No curvature correction anywhere (bandgap or readout); the two-point residual above
  is what remains.
- Layout: the MIM plates' own parasitics, the n-well capacitances and the fill squares' coupling are
  not in the extracted netlist (kpex 0.3.12 limits and behaviour, see "Post-layout" and "Fill");
  wiring resistance is a hand estimate; no post-layout corner or mismatch run; density and antenna
  at chip level are the padring's checks.
- The −3.7 % post-layout gain shift is a simulated number; the bench sees it together with the
  process spread (±15 % from the `rppd` corner) and removes all of it in the two-point
  calibration.
