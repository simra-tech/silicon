## What it is

A relaxation oscillator whose frequency is proportional to absolute temperature. It
reuses the bandgap's ΔV<sub>BE</sub> core: `G1_BGR` biases its HBT pair (1 unit against
8 parallel units, `npn13G2`) and converts ΔV<sub>BE</sub> across R1 (`rppd`) into
I<sub>PTAT</sub>; this block copies that current on the shared mirror rails (`pbias`,
`pcasc`) and charges two `cmim` capacitors alternately against a threshold derived from
V<sub>REF</sub>:

f = I<sub>PTAT</sub> / (2 C V<sub>th</sub>) = (kT/q) ln 8 / (2 R1 C V<sub>REF</sub>)

Schematic `xschem/g1_t2f.sch`, netlist `xschem/g1_t2f.spice` (generated by
`xschem/gen_g1_t2f.py`, netlisted with `xschem -n -q -x`):

- Three cascoded PMOS legs on the bandgap rails (same W/L 10/4 µm devices as the
  bandgap mirror, cascode bodies tied to source): I<sub>PTAT</sub> to the capacitor
  steering node, 2·I<sub>PTAT</sub> into R<sub>REF</sub> (`rppd` 126 kΩ), and
  I<sub>PTAT</sub> into an NMOS diode that biases the comparator tails.
- Two capacitors C1, C2 (`cap_cmim` 28 × 28 µm, 1.18 pF each). PMOS switches steer
  I<sub>PTAT</sub> into one while an NMOS switch holds the other discharged, so the
  reset time does not enter the period.
- Two comparators, one per capacitor: `npn13G2` differential pair on an 8 µA PTAT tail
  (constant g<sub>m</sub>, so the delay is nearly temperature independent), PMOS mirror
  load, two HV inverters. A NAND RS latch (`q`, `qn`) flips at each crossing; `en` = 0
  resets it (3-input NAND) and discharges both capacitors.
- Threshold multiplexer `mode`: 0 → V<sub>th</sub> = V<sub>REF</sub> (PTAT reading);
  1 → V<sub>th</sub> = 2 I<sub>PTAT</sub> R<sub>REF</sub> (reference reading,
  f = 1/(4 R<sub>REF</sub> C), temperature independent to first order).
- Output: `q` is shifted to the 1.2 V domain with two HV NMOS pull-downs and a
  cross-coupled LV PMOS pair (no static current) and buffered by two LV inverters to
  `fout`, which drives the `TEMP_OUT` pad (`sg13g2_IOPadOut16mA`). No divider is
  needed: 1.2 to 2.5 MHz over −40 .. 175 °C.

Pins: `vdd (3.3 V) vdd12 (1.2 V) vss pbias pcasc vref en mode fout`. Control bits and
their register mapping: `INTERFACE.md`.

### Ratio readout

The comparator delay t<sub>d</sub> adds to each half period: T = 2 (C V<sub>th</sub>/I + t<sub>d</sub>).
At 27 °C the simulated overshoot of the capacitor above the threshold is about 35 mV,
i.e. t<sub>d</sub> ≈ 10 ns on a 310 ns half period (3 %). Because the tail current is
PTAT, t<sub>d</sub> is nearly constant and the two-point fit absorbs most of it; the
residual is in the tables below. The REF mode uses the same current, the same
capacitors and the same comparators with only the threshold changed, so
f<sub>PTAT</sub>/f<sub>REF</sub> = 2 ΔV<sub>BE</sub> R<sub>REF</sub> / (R1 V<sub>REF</sub>)
cancels C, the absolute threshold and, to first order, t<sub>d</sub>. Only the `rppd`
ratio R<sub>REF</sub>/R1 and the bandgap quantities remain. In the frozen schematic
R<sub>REF</sub> is 0.5 µm wide and the bandgap's R1 1 µm wide (same type and, in layout, the
same orientation), so the ratio carries the width-dependent part of the `rppd` sheet
resistance as a fixed factor; the two-point calibration of the ratio removes it. This is the twin-oscillator / voltage-ratio idea in
time-multiplexed form; the digital side only has to count both modes with the same
gate.

## Source

None on SG13G2 for this block. The bandgap it hangs on is `../g1_bgr` (see its
"Source" for the 2AMLogic lineage). The two-capacitor alternating relaxation scheme and
the reference-oscillator ratio follow published T2F sensors (Xu et al., Sensors 2023;
the ratio principle of Souri, Chae, Makinwa, JSSC 2013); no text or figure is copied.

| REF | 1.6115 | 1.5967 | 1.5702 | −43103 | −12.93 | 1.0315 / 1.0417 / 1.0626 |

Equivalent °C/V uses f ∝ T (300 K at 27 °C). 1.2 V domain (output level shifter only):
f PTAT = 1.5906 MHz at 1.08, 1.20 and 1.32 V, i.e. no sensitivity.

**This is the block's main weakness: −9.3 °C/V in PTAT mode**, so a 100 mV supply
change during a measurement reads as about 1 °C, and a part must be calibrated and read
at the same `IOVDD`. The numbers point at the threshold node: with the bandgap alone
V<sub>REF</sub> is 1.03786 V at all three supplies (`../g1_bgr/README.md`), and in REF mode
(comparator bases disconnected from `vref`) it stays there, but in PTAT mode V<sub>REF</sub>
rises by +1 / +8 / +23 mV at 3.0 / 3.3 / 3.6 V, and in REF mode V<sub>th</sub> = 2 I<sub>PTAT</sub> R<sub>REF</sub>
rises by the same current into 126 kΩ (+31 mV). A current of roughly 0.25 µA that grows
steeply with V<sub>DD</sub> therefore flows *out of* the comparator bases into the
threshold node. The comparator HBTs sit with their collectors at V<sub>DD</sub> − |V<sub>GS</sub>|
of the PMOS load (about 2.5 V) and their emitters at V<sub>th</sub> − V<sub>BE</sub> (about
0.35 V), i.e. V<sub>CE</sub> ≈ 2.2 V, above the 1.6 V V<sub>CE,max</sub> of the `npn13G2`
model card; avalanche base current is the consistent explanation (the same mechanism the
bandgap removed with its collector cascodes). This mechanism has **not** been isolated by a
dedicated run; the schematic is frozen with it, and the fix (a collector cascode or a
lower-sitting load in the comparator) is recorded under "Unverified" for the next revision.

(Metal3, vdd/vss as north/south bars, 1.6 × 0.8 µm signal stubs) follows `../g1_bgr`, so the two
macros can share the IOVDD/IOVSS straps of `../g1_padring/INTEGRATION.md` step 5
(`PDN_MACRO_CONNECTIONS: ["i_core.<inst> IOVDD IOVSS vdd vss"]`, custom `PDN_CFG` via stacks
onto the bars); `vdd12` additionally needs a VDD strap. `pbias`, `pcasc`, `vref` come from the
bandgap's east-edge pins: placing `g1_t2f` east of `g1_bgr` gives 15 .. 20 µm connections
(`pbias` at y = 68.1 here against 104.05 there, so the two macros are not simply abutted).
`vref`, `pbias`, `pcasc` are high-impedance nets and belong on `RSZ_DONT_TOUCH_RX`; `en` and
`mode` are 3.3 V levels from the analog wrapper's level shifters; `fout` is a 1.2 V CMOS output
(IO2: 1/0.13 + 2/0.13) sized for the ~50 fF of core wiring plus the `sg13g2_IOPadOut16mA` input.

### Guard rings, wells, routing

- Substrate: one p+ ring around the block (vss); the comparator tails, the switch row and the HV
  logic row have full-length p+ tap strips under their NMOS (their vss rails), and every HBT
  sits in its PCell's own contacted p+ ring, the six rings merged into one grid tied to the outer
  ring on the west. Latch-up rules LU.a/LU.b are met by the tap strips in every well and row.
- n-wells: mirror row, load row, switch row and logic row each in one well with an n+ tie strip
  on vdd (the row's vdd rail); the three cascodes each in their own well with the tie strip on
  the cascode's source node; the 1.2 V output stage in its own well on `vdd12`, 2.7 µm from the
  HV row's well (rule NW.b1 1.8 µm).
- The HBT row's collector buried layer keeps 2.2 µm of PWell to the load n-well (rule NBL.d) and
  1.0 µm to the tails' n+ Activ (NBL.e); these two spacings set the block height.
- Routing: Metal1 inside cells and for rails; Metal2 stubs straight down from every cell pin to a
  Metal3 track (the CMOS rows use the cell's own column grid: strips at even, gates at odd
  0.44 µm columns); the NAND series stacks are ordered as in the schematic (input `a` next to the
  output) because the LVS compare is strict about series order. The 1.2 V cells (l = 0.13 and
  0.3 µm) have their S/D strips only 0.255 / 0.34 µm from the gate, so in that row no Metal1
  runs through the gate-contact gap: drains are joined on Metal2 and the gate pads leave sideways
  on Metal1 (`lv_inv` in the generator).
- Sensitive nodes: `cap1`/`cap2` run on one Metal2 column each from the TopVia1 island through
  the switch cell to the HBT base (about 35 µm); `vth` is a Metal3 track in the analog channel
  between the mux and the QA bases; `isrc` and `vr` are current-source nodes; the digital signals
  `d1g`/`d2g` cross the analog channel on their own columns (west of QA1 and between the
  comparator and mirror blocks). The extracted capacitances of these nets are in "Post-layout".

### Extractor view (LVS reference `layout/g1_t2f_lvs.cdl` against `xschem/g1_t2f.spice`)

`layout/make_lvs_cdl.py` rewrites the xschem netlist for the KLayout LVS deck, which reads
devices as `M`/`Q`/`R`/`C` elements, not as `X` subcircuit calls: `XMPO … sg13_hv_pmos … mm_ok=1`
→ `MMPO … sg13_hv_pmos w= l= ng= m=`; `XQA1 … npn13G2 Nx=1` → `QQA1 … npn13G2 le=900n we=70n Nx=1`
(the PCell's emitter, not used by the model); `XRREF vr vss sub! rppd` → `RRREF vr vss vss rppd`
(the layout's substrate is the vss net); `XC1 cap1 vss cap_cmim` → `CC1 cap1 vss cap_cmim`
(first terminal = TopMetal1 plate); and the two dummy HBTs of the comparator row are appended
(`QQD1/QQD2 vss vss vss vss npn13G2`). In the extracted netlist (`reports/lvs/g1_t2f_extracted.cir`)
MT1, MT2, MPR and MCR appear as two 10 µm fingers each (merged to W = 20 µm by the deck's
simplify step), the dummies as one `m=2` device, R<sub>REF</sub> as one 245 µm element, and
every internal net carries its schematic name (Metal2/Metal3 text labels; they become extra
top-level ports of the extracted subcircuit, which the strict-port compare tolerates). All 42
nets and 65 devices (57 MOS after finger merging, 5 HBT entries, R<sub>REF</sub>, C1, C2) match
on both sides (`reports/lvs/g1_t2f.lvsdb`); the compare passed after one wiring fix (the MLA2
diode rail did not reach its drain strip) and the NAND series-order change.

## Post-layout (kpex 2.5D, capacitances)

PEX: `kpex 0.3.12`, engine `--2.5D --mode CC --blackbox true`, on `layout/g1_t2f.gds` with the
LVS CDL as schematic (`reports/pex/cc/kpex_plain.log`, `kpex_stdout.log`):

```
G1_WORKDIR=build/g1_t2f_lay flow/run.sh kpex --pdk ihp_sg13g2 --gds .../layout/g1_t2f.gds --cell g1_t2f \
    --schematic .../layout/g1_t2f_lvs.cdl --2.5D --mode CC --blackbox true --out_dir ...
```

Two limitations of the tool, both visible in the logs: (1) the kpex technology file maps the
MIM plate layer to an unnamed layer (`cmim_top` → `<TODO>`), and without `--blackbox true` the
2.5D engine stops with `KeyError: '<TODO>'`; with it the `cap_cmim` device geometry is excluded
from the field extraction, so the netlist carries the wiring and tab parasitics of `cap1`/`cap2`
but no parasitic of the MIM plates themselves, and (2) kpex's SPICE writer stops at the first
`cap_cmim` device (`Invalid parameter name: 'C'`) after all MOS, HBT and resistor devices have been
written. `sim/postlayout/make_pex_netlist.py` therefore takes the 77 devices from the partial
`reports/pex/cc/g1_t2f_k25d_pex_netlist.spice`, the 340 capacitances from
`g1_t2f_k25d_pex_netlist.csv` (438 fF in total, 340 fF after the substrate-node merge into vss)
and adds C1/C2 back as the schematic devices; the result is `sim/postlayout/g1_t2f_pex.spice`
(format changes only, see the docstring). Largest parasitic loads: `nbias` 32 fF, `pcasc` 28 fF,
`pbias` 27 fF, **`cap1` 25 fF, `cap2` 26 fF** (2.1 % of the 1.18 pF timing capacitors), `en` 23 fF,
`d2g` 22 fF, `d1g` 14 fF, `vr` 12 fF, `vth` 12 fF, `isrc` 12 fF, the comparator outputs
`ob1`/`ob2` 6.6 fF each.

`sim/postlayout/run_postlayout.py all` runs the block testbench (`../tb_g1_t2f.spice.tmpl` with
the two `.include` lines exchanged: `tb_g1_t2f_pex.spice.tmpl`, bandgap in its schematic view so
that only this block's layout enters) at −40, 25, 27, 100, 125, 175 °C (PTAT) and −40, 27, 125,
175 °C (REF) and at 3.0/3.3/3.6 V; `compare.py` writes `sim/postlayout/results/compare.md`:

- **The layout costs a −3.6 .. −3.8 % gain shift in PTAT mode, constant over temperature, and
  leaves the calibrated accuracy where it was**: the two-point residual moves by at most 0.10 °C
  (at −40 °C). The shift has two parts, both proportional to absolute frequency: the 25 fF of
  wiring on each timing node (−2.1 %) and the comparator delay, which grows from about 10 to
  14 ns (overshoot 36 → 48 mV) because the comparator outputs, the inverter chain, the latch and
  above all the steering-gate nets `d1g`/`d2g` (14 .. 22 fF) are now loaded (−1.2 %). In REF mode
  the ten resistor heads of R<sub>REF</sub> add about +1 % (35 Ω·µm per head at 0.5 µm width,
  18 extra heads, 1.3 kΩ on 126.9 kΩ), giving −4.0 .. −4.9 %; the ratio f<sub>PTAT</sub>/f<sub>REF</sub>
  therefore rises by 0.2 .. 1.4 %, most at −40 °C where the delay is the largest share of the
  period.
- The supply sensitivity worsens slightly (−9.3 → −9.8 °C/V PTAT): the mechanism of the schematic
  (threshold-node loading by the comparator HBTs, see "Supply") is unchanged and the delay term
  adds its own V<sub>DD</sub> dependence.
- **Wiring resistance is estimated, not extracted.** The kpex RC mode was not run: the bandgap
  track found (their README, `../g1_bgr/reports/pex/rc/`) that kpex 0.3.12 leaves the device
  instances off the resistor trees of internal nets, so an RC netlist has a singular operating
  point. A hand count with the LVS deck's sheet resistances (Metal1 0.110, Metal2/Metal3
  0.088 Ω/sq) and 20 Ω per via cut (PDK LEF, the bandgap track's value): `vr` from the
  R<sub>REF</sub> head to the MCR drains ≈ 105 Ω (48 µm of 0.2 µm Metal2 riser, 13 µm of track,
  four cuts) in series with 126.9 kΩ (+0.08 %); `isrc` ≈ 95 Ω at 4 µA (0.4 mV on a cascode
  output, no effect on the current); `cap1`/`cap2` ≈ 70 Ω in series with 1.18 pF (80 fs); the
  tails' vss rail 2.4 Ω at 20 µA (50 µV); the vdd stripe 3 Ω at 60 µA (0.2 mV). Nothing exceeds
  0.1 % of a design quantity, so no wiring-resistance variant was simulated.
- Not in the post-layout netlist: the MIM plates' own parasitics (blackboxed, see above; the
  TopMetal1 plate edge and the Metal5 plate to substrate are absorbed into vss and the cap model
  is the PDK's area value), the well-to-substrate capacitances of the n-wells (the 2.5D engine
  has no well conductor), and the bandgap's own layout (its post-layout V<sub>REF</sub> is +1.4 mV,
  `../g1_bgr/README.md`, i.e. −0.14 % on f in PTAT mode).

## Unverified

- 77 K: the −196 °C run extrapolates the PDK models. Published measurements on an
  SG13G2 HBT show the ideality factor rising to about 1.35 .. 1.4 at 77 K (T<sub>eff</sub>
  ≈ 105 K) and the I–V family collapsing below ~60 K, so a linear PTAT reading at 77 K
  will read high by roughly +28 K and the sensitivity vanishes below ~60 K. The
  calibrated range is −40 .. 150 °C (QFN limit); 77 K is a characterisation point for
  n(T), not a temperature reading. No accuracy is claimed there.
- Package stress shift of V<sub>BE</sub> and ΔV<sub>BE</sub> after QFN assembly:
  calibrate packaged parts, not wafers.
- Comparator offset and its drift (HBT pair, no MC run), base-current loading of the
  threshold node (about 27 nA into 88 kΩ at V<sub>REF</sub>, 126 kΩ at V<sub>R</sub>;
  β-dependent, hence not PTAT), charge injection of the steering switches, and the
  `cmim` temperature coefficient are all inside the simulated numbers but not
  separately bounded.
- The comparator HBTs operate at V<sub>CE</sub> ≈ 2.2 V, above the 1.6 V V<sub>CE,max</sub> of
  the model card (the load is a PMOS diode from V<sub>DD</sub>, the emitters sit at
  V<sub>th</sub> − V<sub>BE</sub>). The supply sensitivity numbers point at avalanche base
  current into the threshold node (see "Supply"); the mechanism has not been isolated by a
  dedicated run and the reliability margin of `npn13G2` at V<sub>CE</sub> = 2.2 V is not known
  from the PDK. A collector cascode in the comparator (as in the bandgap) or a load that sits
  lower would remove both; not done in the frozen schematic. Until then, calibrate and read at
  the same `IOVDD`.
- No curvature correction anywhere (bandgap or readout); the two-point residual above
  is what remains.
- Layout: the MIM plates' own parasitics and the n-well capacitances are not in the extracted
  netlist (kpex 0.3.12 limits, see "Post-layout"); wiring resistance is a hand estimate; no
  post-layout corner or mismatch run; density and antenna at chip level are the padring's checks.
- The −3.7 % post-layout gain shift is a simulated number; the bench sees it together with the
  process spread (±15 % from the `rppd` corner) and removes all of it in the two-point
  calibration.
