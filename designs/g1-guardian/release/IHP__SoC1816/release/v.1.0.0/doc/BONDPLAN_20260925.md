## Package and orientation

- **Package:** QFN24, 4 × 4 mm body, 0.5 mm lead pitch, six leads per side, exposed paddle.
- **Lead numbering (top view):** counter-clockwise. Lead 1 is the top lead of the left side,
  next to the top-left corner, which carries the **pin-1 mark**. Left side: leads 1–6, top to
  bottom. Bottom: 7–12, left to right. Right side: 13–18, bottom to top. Top: 19–24, right to left.
- **Die orientation:** the die sits centred on the paddle, **rotated 90° clockwise** from the
  GDS view. The GDS south edge (pads 1–6) faces leads 1–6. The GDS east edge (pads 7–12) faces leads 7–12.
  The GDS north edge (pads 13–18) faces leads 13–18. The GDS west edge (pads 19–24) faces leads 19–24.
  Die pad 1 (`VDD`) is the top pad of the left die edge, facing lead 1.
- **Paddle:** exposed paddle connected to `VSS`. Conductive die attach, so the substrate
  (core `VSS`) is tied to the paddle. The paddle is soldered to the board `VSS` plane. There
  are no down-bonds: all 24 pads bond to leads. On the board, leads 2 and 5 (`VSS`) and
  4 and 6 (`IOVSS`) tie to the same ground as the paddle.
- **Opposite lead:** the lead of the facing package side with the same rank along the side,
  counted in the counter-clockwise direction. The pads are at 112 µm pitch and the leads at
  500 µm, so "opposite" means the same rank, not the same coordinate.

## Reversed order on the north and west edges

Going counter-clockwise around the die, pads 1→6 (south, left to right in the GDS) and
7→12 (east, bottom to top) run in the same direction as the leads. Pads 13→18 (north) run left to
right and pads 19→24 (west) run bottom to top: both are **clockwise**. Bonding each pad to the lead opposite
it therefore gives the reversed order on those two edges:

- pads 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 → leads 18, 17, 16, 15, 14, 13
- pads 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 → leads 24, 23, 22, 21, 20, 19

The alternative, bonding pad *n* to lead *n*, gives 30 wire crossings
(15 on each of the two edges; the generator counts them). The chosen plan has
**0 crossings**.

## What the bonding house still has to confirm

- Pad opening 65.8 × 65.8 µm at 112 µm pitch, 68.1 µm from the die edge (bonding-rule
  acceptance: **not run**).
- The die thickness of 200 µm and the conductive die attach.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, in the pinned container:

```sh
G1_CPUSET=<cpu> G1_CPUS=1 G1_CONTAINER_ENGINE=podman G1_WORKDIR=. flow/run.sh klayout -b \
  -r designs/g1-guardian/padframe/bondplan_20260925.py \
  -rd gds=designs/g1-guardian/blocks/g1_padring/layout/g1_chip_top_1414_r3.gds \
  -rd src_csv=designs/g1-guardian/padframe/bondmap_candidate_20260923_r2.csv \
  -rd out_csv=designs/g1-guardian/padframe/bondmap_20260926_r4.csv \
  -rd out_svg=designs/g1-guardian/padframe/bondplan_20260925.svg \
  -rd out_json=designs/g1-guardian/padframe/bondplan_20260925.json \
  -rd gds_sha=7d07a7841a531f51e08b0c90e76fe603889cd2ef29905e309e63f09742e688f2
```

The script refuses any GDS other than the expected SHA-256 (`gds_sha`; the default is r2
`9049e87b…`, which produced `bondmap_20260925_r3.csv`). Against r2, the r3 run changed only the
hash column of the CSV, the hash in the SVG title and the path and hash lines of the JSON.
Lead assignment, wire lengths and the 0 crossings are unchanged. It asserts that every
CSV row coincides with exactly one Passiv opening and that every TopMetal2 label at an opening
equals the row's net.
