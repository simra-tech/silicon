named. G1 geometry was read from `designs/g1-guardian/blocks/g1_padring/layout/g1_chip_top_1414_r4.gds`
with a small GDS stream parser (KLayout is not installed on the host).

Verdict: as first written it does not fit; the cut list alone (−0.19 mm²) does not close it. Closing on the
1.414 mm die needs cuts 1–4, compact new BGR and sense cells, the iterative digital block and either one ADC
with dual track-and-hold or ADCs ≤ 0.06 mm². Alternative: 1.53 mm die for the lean variant (2.35 mm²), 1.81 mm
for the plan as written (3.3 mm²); QFN24 accepts up to 2 × 2 mm.

## Pin map proposal (24)

The first list counted 26. Share `MA`=`SCLK`, `SLO`=`SDO` (CS low selects SPI; no SPI during BiSS frames; SDO
needs a TriOut cell if bused). Drop `TEMP_OUT` for an `EN`/`STO_IN` input, which the first list lacked. The second
encoder track (4 pins) and ABZ (3 pins) were never in the 26, so cuts 1 and 2 are forced by pins.

| Side | Pad | Pin | `sg13g2_io` cell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S | 1–6 | VDD, VSS, IOVDD, IOVSS, VSS, IOVSS | IOPadVdd / Vss / IOVdd / IOVss |
| E | 7 | VDDA | IOPadAnalog bare terminal (G1 D14: no legal `iovdd` tap into the core) |
| E | 8–11 | ISA+, ISA−, ISB+, ISB− | IOPadAnalog bare |
| E | 12 | FAULT_N | IOPadOut4mA |
| N | 13–15 | GATE_A/B/C | IOPadOut30mA |
| N | 16 | CS | IOPadIn |
| N | 17 | MA/SCLK | IOPadIn |
| N | 18 | SLO/SDO | IOPadOut4mA or TriOut4mA |
| W | 19 | SDI | IOPadIn |
| W | 20 | EN/STO_IN | IOPadIn |
| W | 21–24 | SIN+, SIN−, COS+, COS− | IOPadAnalog (587 Ω `padres` acceptable for a high-impedance PGA input, estimated) |
