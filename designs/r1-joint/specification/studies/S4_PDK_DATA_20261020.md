Consequence for the SAR CDAC (S1): σ_u/C_u ≤ 0.26 % (DNL ≤ 0.5 LSB) needs a unit of ≥ 14.8 µm² ≈ 22 fF; the 1.5 % INL
limit needs only 0.44 µm² (the DRC minimum 1.30 µm² then applies). A 6+6 bridge array with 128 × 22 fF units is ≈ 2.8 pF
per side, well above the kT/C floor of 0.3 pF, and ≈ 1900 µm² of MIM per side (estimated).
