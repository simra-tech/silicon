## 2. Cut list

If area, pins or schedule do not close, blocks are removed in this order and the README says so:

1. ABZ incremental output (3 pins) — fold into SPI-only reporting.
2. Second encoder track (nonius absolute) — single-track incremental with index.
3. PWM/commutation generator — gate outputs become controller-driven pass-through with safety gating.
4. Second current channel — one channel, single-phase monitoring.
5. T2F temperature output pin — report over the bus only.

What is never cut: the sine/cosine ADC path, the position computation, one serial interface, the
safety channel with the latched fault output, the bandgap.

Lean total after D1–D8: ≈ 0.45–0.50 mm², marginal on the baseline die.

**D10 (decided 2026-10-21: 1.6 mm die; see the closing paragraph).** T5 synthesis puts the digital macro at ≈ 0.34 mm² placed (30.4 kGE; `r1_pos` 36 %, registers 19 %, `r1_gate` 17 %, `r1_safe` 10 %, BiSS 10 %, SPI 5 %), against the 0.18 mm² estimate, which predates the on-chip PWM generator (open item 6). Choices: (a) trim the RTL (shared multiplier in `r1_pos`, fewer writable registers, narrower PWM counters) to ≈ 0.28 mm² and still take the 1.5 mm die (D9); (b) the 1.5 mm die with the RTL as is; (c) cut the PWM generator back to pass-through (cut 3) and the I²t model to the host. Decision after T1–T4 report their analog areas.

**D16 amendment (2026-10-27, ADC full scale).** Post-layout the CDAC bottom-plate wiring (≈ 1.7 pF per slice, layout A and B) attenuates the input: gain 1.058, full scale ±0.864 V against the ±0.9 V of D16. The AFE and isense drive ±0.85 V and do not clip; the linear gain error is removed by the weight calibration (INTERFACES AFE → ADC settling row). Decision: the full-scale limit is ±0.86 V simulated post-layout (1.6 % above the ±0.85 V inputs); a CDAC change to recover ±0.9 V is the fallback if layout B4 shows the margin below 1 % at any corner. Taken by the coordinator as the recommended option; reversible.

**D22 (2026-10-28, encoder channel offset and gain correction).** `r1_ctrl` has no per-channel offset or gain correction for the sine and cosine codes before the CORDIC (register map 0.7: `RAW_SIN`/`RAW_COS` only). The AFE post-layout ×32 output offset is −6 to −25 mV (layout E5) against a 5 mV target; 25 mV on ±0.85 V is about 1.7° of angle error if uncorrected, and sensor offsets of the same order come from the encoder head itself. Decision: `r1_ctrl` 0.8 adds host-written offset and gain words per channel (`SIN_OFS`, `COS_OFS`, `SIN_GAIN`, `COS_GAIN`, applied to the corrected codes before the CORDIC, reset = no correction) and a min/max capture of the raw codes for host calibration; the AFE keeps the 5 mV layout target (one more iteration, resistance-aware balancing of the `x1` routes) and additionally records the offset drift over temperature and supply, which the correction cannot remove, with a limit of 1 mV (≈ 0.07°). Taken by the coordinator as the recommended option; reversible.

**D21 (2026-10-27, ADC SAR logic ownership).** Assembly try f found that the SAR logic of `r1_adc` (`r1_sar5_logic`: per-slice decision latches with P/N flags, CDAC switch control from the decisions, decision-bit and `valid` outputs) exists only as a behavioural XSPICE model in the ADC decks; no block owns a silicon implementation, the ADC layouts A–B3 leave 77 SAR-internal pins open and the 30 ADC outputs to `r1_ctrl` undriven. The ADC → ctrl contract (14 raw decisions + `valid` per slice, strobes and frame timing from ctrl per D18) stays as it is. Decision: the SAR logic becomes a standard-cell sub-macro inside the `r1_adc` macro (RTL derived from the behavioural model, bit-exact against it; hardened with LibreLane in a row at the ADC north edge, ≤ 439 × 15 µm, 1.2 V `vdd`, with the ADC's own ground separation kept), so `r1_ctrl` 0.7 and the chip pin template do not change. ADC layout B5 integrates it; the mixed-cell LVS uses the comparer-limit deck. Taken by the coordinator as the recommended option; reversible.

**D20 (2026-10-26, VREF buffer).** The full-chip testbench (track T10) found that nothing buffers the bandgap output: `r1_bgr` `vref` (≈ 180 kΩ source, qualified with a 1 pF load) drives the `r1_safe` threshold string (4.2 µA) and the `r1_isense` pedestal resistors directly, and the chip-level netlist settles at 0.22 V instead of 1.045 V. The `r1_safe` record had assumed "VREF buffered (ideal source here)" and no block owned the buffer (ownership gap of the same kind as the 2026-10-21 table). Decision: `r1_bias` revision 2 adds a unity-gain reference buffer with the output `vref_b` for `r1_safe` and `r1_isense` (and `r1_t2f` if its `vref` pin draws current), drive ≥ 50 µA into ≥ 20 pF with the output impedance recorded for the consumer decks, offset ≤ 2 mV over PVT and mismatch (one threshold step is 4.17 mV), bias from the existing `r1_bias` legs. The ADC keeps its own path from the raw `vref` through `r1_refbuf5` and its 10 pF filter, which uses the 180 kΩ source as the noise pole. `r1_bgr` is not changed. Consumer decks (safe, isense post-layout) must re-run with the buffer output impedance in place of the ideal source before the chip-level campaign. Taken by the coordinator as the recommended option; reversible.

**D19 (2026-10-21, gate fault-path load).** The fault path is specified as ≤ 1 µs at direct gate loads ≤ 1 nF (simulated 443–933 ns). Loads above 1 nF are not supported directly: a 30 mA `sg13g2` pad needs 0.55 µs alone to discharge 5 nF from 3.3 V, and a stronger driver would need paralleled pads (no pins) or a custom pad with its own ESD design. Real drives use a gate-driver IC (20–100 pF input). The 200 ns figure in the first draft had no load and is withdrawn. Taken by the coordinator as the recommended option; reversible.

**D13 amendment (2026-10-21, oscillator trim-code rule).** Post-layout revision 2 gives 30.30 MHz at reset code 22 (tt), 32.70 MHz at code 0 at ss/1.08 V/125 °C, 39.53 MHz at code 22 and 56.37 MHz at code 0 at ff/1.32 V/−40 °C. Codes 0–17 can exceed the 42 MHz digital STA limit at the fast corner. Rule: the digital STA limit stays 42 MHz; the reset code 22 is below 42 MHz at every corner; the host may write a trim code below 18 only after `OSC_COUNT` shows the resulting frequency ≤ 40 MHz (bench procedure B-trim in `measurement/README.md`); `r1_ctrl` 0.6 documents the rule in the register map and, if cheap, refuses codes below 18 until `OSC_COUNT` has been read once since reset (open item for 0.6).

**D10 amendment (2026-10-21, S7): die 1.7 mm.** With the real macro sizes (`specification/studies/S7_REFLOORPLAN.md`) the laid-out analog sum is 0.342 mm² against the 0.30 mm² trigger, so the escalation rule of the D10 closure applies. The best 1.6 mm candidate (C5) reaches 5.48 % margin only with four unproven conditions (r1_safe re-laid to ≤ 175 × 410 µm, r1_ctrl re-hardened with a pin template, VDDA decoupling moved into the ring band, oscillator at its revision-2 size) and a 1.1 mm `vref` route, before chip fill and IR-drop. The 1.7 mm floorplan (C4) holds every macro as laid out with 13.4 % margin and no new rule. Decision: die 1.7 mm (+12.9 % area over 1.6 mm, +45 % over the G1 die); 24 pads in the same order at the wider pitch; the pending macros keep the S7 slots (ADC ≤ 439 × 182 µm, pins: digital north, AFE inputs west, isense inputs east, supplies south; osc ≤ 154 × 118 µm with all signal pins west; isense ≤ 158 × 297 µm, inputs east, outputs west). Taken by the coordinator under the pre-agreed trigger; reversible until the ring is submitted. The 1.6 mm C5 plan stays recorded as the fallback if the owner rejects the cost.

**D10 closure (2026-10-21, S6).** The S6 floorplan study (`specification/studies/S6_FLOORPLAN.md`) puts the required area at 0.741 mm² against a 0.566 mm² window at 1.414 mm (−31 %); with every cut including the functional ones it is still −10 %. At 1.6 mm the required 0.787 mm² fits the 0.880 mm² window (analog macros under the core ring, as on G1 r4) with 10.6 % margin, given five non-functional cuts: digital at 75 % (C1), register trims to ≤ 240 000 µm² placed (C2), stacked MIM-on-MOS ADC decoupling (C5), AFE MIM over resistors (C6), analog under the ring (C7). Decision: the die is 1.6 mm (ring pitch 140 µm, same 24 pads in the same order); escalate to 1.7 mm if the laid-out analog sum exceeds 0.30 mm² or the digital macro does not close at 75 %. Hard constraint for `r1_ctrl` 0.4: DIE_AREA 520 × 664 µm, ≤ 240 000 µm² placed, 42 MHz at three corners. The functional cuts C3, C4, C8 are rejected.

- **D18 (2026-10-20, ADC schedule, replaces the D17 frame arithmetic).** D17 asked for both pairs in a 36-tick frame with conversion ≤ 17 ticks and a 250 ns track window per slice; with each slice converting twice per frame this forces conversion ≤ 10.5 ticks, which r1_adc revision 3 could only meet with two decisions per tick (13 ns strobe: comparator noise fails at ss/125 °C, an undesigned 3 ns delay cell). ## 4. Design-trace record

Every run of this design is kept for later reuse (training and evaluation tasks derived from real
design sessions). Rules, binding for every agent on R1:

1. Simulation decks, logs, raw waveforms and reports go under the block's `sim/` or `reports/`
   directory, or under the bulk results root (`G1_RESULTS_ROOT`) with a symlink in the tree. Nothing
   that is evidence lives only under `/tmp`.
2. Each run directory carries a `README.md` or `run.json` with the command, tool versions, PDK
   commit, inputs, the question asked, the result and the disposition (passed / failed / not run).
3. Failed and abandoned runs are kept, not deleted; they are labelled as such. A failure trace is as
   useful as a pass.
4. Session and subagent transcripts are copied to durable storage by a scheduled job; agents do not
   need to do this, but must not place artefacts in locations the job does not cover.
5. Large files (GDS, VCD, waveform dumps above a few MB) go to the bulk root, never into git.
